# Film 41 · "Bill Gates Changed His Mind About AI and Jobs" · MOTION (binding)

Thumbnail A: **GATES CHANGED HIS MIND ON AI**, a giant hand of warm light turns its thumb from up to down, a tiny figure in a yellow coat at a desk, night indigo + warm gold.
Rules: `regeln/REGELN.md` §13 (Jakob 24.11.): one picture per sentence, explainer share 60–70 %, **no single big scene with zooms**, a real motion-design film.
Skills, in this order: `scenario-motion` (channel style, wins on conflict), `world-class-motion-design`, `fluid-motion-design`, `motion-director-gates`, plus `dataviz` for every number graphic.
Shot list: `docs/shots.md`, generated by `tools/shots41.py`. Timing: the real VO in `<ROOT>audio/timing.json` (617.5 s; end screen 602.5–617.5).

## 1 · The idea

**Gates' verdict is a hand. Our answer is a list.**
The film opens on the thumbnail: a hand of light turns its thumb down over the night while you sit small at your desk.
The hand is Gates' changed mind. Every graphic after it explains one sentence.
The film's through-line is **THE GOLD THREAD**:
- it draws the 2023 → 2026 axis;
- it rings a block of jobs ("set aside");
- the ringed block folds into the lines of **THE LIST** (HUMAN RESERVED);
- it becomes the timeline NOW · 2–8 YEARS · 20 YEARS;
- at the end it is the empty line of the list that waits for a reason.

The last picture rhymes with the first. The hand is dim in the sky and the list stands in the night beside your desk. The core line asks who gets to write it.

**Form.** This is a locked-frame motion-design film (world-class gate 3, the "graphic 2D" alternative).
- Continuity comes from **morphs, masks, match cuts and carried objects**, never from a camera flying over one big picture.
- A push into the same picture is a moving hold, never a new motif.
- Scenes (you at your desk, the sky, the site, the jury, the tower, the far end) are separate compositions. They come back as anchors, built fresh each time.

## 2 · Motion DNA (cell: calm × sharp, "premium editorial")

| dial | choice |
|---|---|
| tempo | calm narration, 1 new graphic per sentence (median ≈ 3 s), quick accents only on key words |
| easing | entrances `expoOut` (0.4–0.8 s), exits `expoIn` (0.3–0.5 s), moves `quartIO`; never linear except conveyors and drifts |
| base unit | 0.4 s; stagger 40 ms in lists, 20–30 ms in grids, ordered from the point of impulse (centre-out, along the move) |
| speed contrast | slow holds (drift, push 1–5 %) against fast morphs (0.6–1 s); one hard accent per ~15 s |
| physics | light and precise; overshoot only on the thumb turn, the switch wobble and the ring recoil |
| shapes | flat light shapes in the editorial-night look (f10): tiles, lines, cards, the hand; grain only as texture |
| type | heavy condensed caps (Inter Black, `F.say` / `F.heavy`) for statements, Instrument Serif for quotes, Inter 600 spaced caps for labels and sources (`F.label`, ≥ 22 px) |
| transitions | 1 morph (shape becomes shape), 2 carried object (the hand, your tile, the knob, the gold thread), 3 match cut on a rect (tile → window → card → clip plate) |
| signature | the thumb turn; the gold thread drawing a ring |
| wow | one ★ per chunk (shots.md); the film's wow is H.13 (the ring sets work aside) |

## 4 · The vocabulary (lib/f41_kit.mjs; scenes in lib/f41_world.mjs)

- `F.stage(ctx, t)`: the night stage under every graphic.
- `F.say(ctx, t, [[word, tSpoken], …], x, y, o)`: kinetic type. Words rise as spoken, flash gold on their word and sink out (`o.out`).
- `F.heavy` / `F.label`: one-piece lines.
- `F.tile(ctx, t, x, y, s, o)`: one job.
  - Colour: amber, `cold`, `gold`, `dim`.
  - Effects: a passing `flash`, `glyph` 'screen' | 'hand' | 'person' | 'you', `lift`, own brightness `v`.
  - `F.field(cols, rows, s, g, cx, cy)` gives the grid.
- `F.hand(ctx, t, wrist x, y, L, { turn 0→1, lum, ghost, arrow, arm })`: Gates' verdict. `arm 1.1` = the small icon with a short cuff.
- `F.list(…)`: HUMAN RESERVED.
  - It rises, the head writes itself, lines take text, one line waits lit (`lit`), and cold lines can fill it from the bottom (`fill`).
  - `S.juryList` is the c07/c08 state.
- `F.timeline(ctx, t, T, { big })`: the clock. It changes **by itself** from `F.TIMELINE` phrases. Draw it in every frame from c02 on, except inside clips.
- `F.yourWindow(ctx, t, box, { u, look, glass })` and `F.desk(…)`: you at your desk (the thumbnail figure).
- `F.lever(…)`: the switch (H.12, 3.11).
- `F.goldSpan(…)`: the gold thread.
- `F.quoteCard`, `F.dataCard`, `F.paperCard`, `F.verdict`: quote and number cards.
- `F.portrait` + `F.nameplate`: the Commons portrait print, used ONCE, with `F.source(…, F.photoCredit())`.
- `F.clipPlate(ctx, t, T, id, { from, morph, dark, label })`: the clip window (C1 FT Live, C2/C3 Decoder). It grows out of a rect and goes back into one.
- `F.subscribe(…)`: the calm cream pill with a gold ring.
- Scenes:
  - `S.sky` (thumbnail A);
  - `S.machinePic`, `S.gapChart` (the −19 % chart, base for 2.05);
  - plates `S.plateMachine`, `S.plateNumbers`, `S.plateJury`;
  - `S.endScreen`.
- `F.SOURCES`: every on-screen string, fact-checked against vo-v4 and clips-geprueft.json. Use these strings, do not retype them.

## 5 · Hard rules (each shot, before render)

1. **One picture per sentence**: it shows exactly what is said (number, quote, comparison, mechanism, person, place). The words on screen are the key words of the sentence, timed to the spoken word (±2 frames): `F.say`.
2. **Nothing from nowhere**: every new element grows out of, is carried by, or morphs from something on screen. Write the carrier into the code comment.
3. **No crossfade, no slide preset, no cut to black.** Text rises and sinks behind its own baseline mask.
4. At most 1–2 things in active motion at once; the rest drift. Nothing ever stands still (frame difference > 0).
5. Text ≥ 22 px, ≤ 6 words per statement + one number (quotes verbatim). Nothing important below y 918 (bottom 15 %: subtitles).
6. Gates' face only in the clips and the one Commons portrait print. No AI image, no drawn likeness. Clips carry ORIGINAL FOOTAGE next to the source.
7. Don't reuse earlier films' motifs: no clock face, ladders or rungs, stamps, price tags or receipts, a great hall, doors, a pen, chess, the long left→right land.
   No robots as heroes: the c04 humanoids stay small and distant. No QR, no logos, no red.
8. Explainer share is measured, not felt: `python3 tools/shots41.py` (target 60–70 %; now 67.7 % on the real VO). Change the shot list first, then the code.
