## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise: long soft eases (sineIO for glides, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0, a few hard accents on the key word (≤ 2 per 30 s: the jolt in H.10, a door jolt, a lever touch), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s; big view changes ≥ 1.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph** — one object becomes the next (door opening → box, point → card, tag → mini tag, plate → cell); (2) **fly-through** — the camera flies into a door slit and the room lives inside it (and back out the same way); (3) **carry** — an object leaves one picture and lands in the next (the eye and the chip fly into HUD pockets and come back stations later).
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects fold back into the door they came from, or fly to a pocket; never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Overlay technique (sc01):** overlays are designed in screen px at a reference view and drawn in yard coordinates (`ref`, `inRef`, `refPt` in sc01), so they ride the camera like objects on the ground.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms (reading order, or outward from the impulse); a group ≤ ~0.8 s.
- **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things; ambient (pool breathing, door light flicker, figure breathing, the global camera float) does not count.
- **Camera:** views `{ x, y, s }` through `F.viewPath(t, [[t, view], …])`; it moves only with a reason (follow a carrier, push into it, pull back for the reveal). `film.mjs cameraFloat` keeps every frame alive; the seam plates creep 4 %/s.
- **Typography:** statements Instrument Serif with a warm glow (`F.big`, `F.goldText`), labels Inter caps ≥ 22 px (`F.label`), sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`), ≤ 6 words + one number. Nothing important below y 880 (subtitles).
- **The counter HUD** (top right): ENDING n/12 rolls on the station phrases, RANKING: OURS, NOT TEGMARK'S, twelve mini doors with their tag dots, pockets for the eye (door 4), the chip, the key (door 7). `F.counterHud(ctx, t, T)` in every yard frame; it hides itself in clip windows and outside the counter phrases. Never set by hand.

## 4 · Colour code (meaning, never violate)
