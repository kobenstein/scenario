# Film 26 · "Why Elon Says Saving Money Is Pointless" · motion bible (lead, 2026-11-11)

Binding for every chunk. Style: `/mnt/project-files/scenario/stil/STIL-editorial-nacht.md` (editorial night, flat grain shapes, one warm hero light, small faceless figures). Shot list: `docs/shots.md` (generated by `tools/shots26.py`). Kit: `lib/f26_kit.mjs`, places + seams: `lib/f26_seams.mjs`. Reference build: `scenes/sc01.mjs`. No AI images in the film: everything is drawn in code.

## 1 · Intent

A calm, fair check of one claim. Musk says saving is pointless because machines will make everything cheap. The film does not mock him: it writes his claim down as **five lines on a test card** and checks them one by one in a world the viewer knows (your window, your street, the shop, the mailbox). Result: ✓ ½ ✗ ✗ ✗, and "as advice, it fails its own test". The ending is warm: saving is the bridge to that future, and a law (not a promise) is what made old age safe once before. Audience: people with a savings account, not investors. Mood: **calm, clear, warm**.

## 2 · The world (one city at night)

One camera space, two places in front of the same sky and the same far city (`F.sky`, `F.city`):

- **THE HILL** (guiding image = thumbnail A; used only as an anchor: the hook, the question, line 5 in c08, the ending in c11). A giant indigo **piggy bank** on a hill above the river, cracked open on top; coins rise out of the crack and turn into gold dust; **you** (yellow coat) small at its feet. Across the river the city and an **unfinished bridge** (piers with lamps, no deck in the middle). In c11 the gold dust builds the deck: "saving is the bridge to it" (`hillWorld(…, { deck })`).
- **YOUR STREET** (Jakob 8.11.: more street and city). House A with **your window** (your room: the wall calendar, the lamp, the small whole piggy on the sill, you / the older you), house B with the **ON AIR window** upstairs (the podcast studio; every clip plate grows out of its monitor or out of another screen in the world) and the **shop window** below (goods, price tags). The **mailbox** at the curb, street lamps in front. Later the street becomes the **avenue of years** (line 4: years on the lamp posts, the date that moves) and, in c09/c10, the **old street** (1889/1935, wooden mailbox, Ida May Fuller's check No. 1).
- **THE SKY FIELD**: the explainers live in the night sky above the roofs and are made of film things only: coins, coin stacks (price comparisons), paper price tags on strings, gold-dust lines, paper checks, law pages, serif numbers, quote cards, the balance. Never generic chart boxes.
- **THE TEST CARD** (the carrier of the whole film, `F.testCard`): born in the hook from three quote cards, written in c02 (4 FORECAST lines + 1 ADVICE line), then in the top right corner until c11. At every station its line lifts out as the big headline and comes back with its mark (pen strokes, never stamps): ✓ gold · ½ gold · ✗ ✗ ✗ rose. Final: big in the centre, ADVICE ✗ FAILS ITS OWN TEST, then back to the corner and away.

**Doors.** W = light door (dive into a light, flood, out of the matching light) or a match cut: only 3 in the film (1.02 far window → your lamp, 9.01 archive → 1889, 10.06 old mailbox → your mailbox). S = sky door: rise until only stars + far-city tops are in view (`S.SKY_Y`, cam.y ≤ −1500 at s ≈ 1), switch the near layers, come down over the other place. Both places share `SKY_GLOW` and the city, so the frame at the switch is identical by construction. A visible carrier crosses every sky door (your piggy in 1.08, a coin in 1.13). Window fly-ins (push through a lit window into a room and out again) are continuous moves, neither W nor S.

## 3 · Nicht wiederholen (do not repeat earlier films)

No domino rows or rank stones (#25), no umbrella (#19), no seal / certificate / rubber stamp (#5), no monolith, no clock or dial, no hourglass, no fire circle, no staircase of light, no map with pins, no running track, no kitchen table with generations, no server rack. ## 4 · Motion DNA

| | |
|---|---|
| Energy | calm, documentary; long glides (2–4 s) against few hard accents (≤ 2 per 30 s): a coin landing, a mark struck, a sign lighting |
| Easing | sine in-out for camera glides (`F.camPath` glides through its keys), expo out for arrivals of things, expo in only into a light (dives) |
| Physics | coins are heavy and spin (`F.coin` flip); dust is weightless and drifts; the balance beam is heavy (expo out + small settle) |
| Shapes | flat 2.5D cards at depth (`E.card` at Z), parallax from the camera, grain finish `F.PRINT26` |
| Transitions | carrier handoffs: a coin flies into the next place, dust leads the camera, the test card line lifts and returns; doors as above; never a crossfade |
| Type | Instrument Serif for quotes and numbers, Inter 600 caps for labels and lines, the title face only for STOP SAVING MONEY |
| Camera | `F.camPath` (glides through keys), `E.camAt` with `'i'`/`'o'` only for light doors; nothing ever stands still (plates creep 3 %/s) |
| Signature | the test card: one line lifts out, the world answers, the mark lands, the line goes home |

## 5 · Palette and colour code (never break it)

- **Night** (indigo/blue, `P.night*`, `P.ind*`, `P.blue*`): the world, the ground of every frame. Always.
- **Gold** (`F.GOLD`, `F.COIN`): money, what stays, the hero light, ✓ and ½. One warm hero light per frame.
- **Rose** (`F.ROSE.mark`): only ✗ and what fails (sparingly: a mark, a verdict, a blinking empty field on a check). Never a fill colour for big areas.
- **Cool** (`F.COOL`): the machine (rovers, machine light). Never on people.
- **Yellow coat**: only "you" (`E.du`, `F.older`). Everybody else is an indigo silhouette (`E.person`), faceless.

## 6 · Calm rules (all chunks)

1. One idea per sentence (the shot list), one main movement at a time; the next move starts before the last one ends.
2. Nothing appears from nowhere and nothing fades in half-transparent: things rise out of a mask (`maskLine`, `rise`), grow out of something already there (a coin flips into a card, a monitor grows into the clip plate), or are carried in.
3. Causality: a reaction starts 40–120 ms after its cause (the window lights when the coin lands, the sign when the coin hits it).
4. Text on screen ≥ 1.5 s, only the strings of `docs/shots.md` §5; quotes keep their quote marks only when verbatim (`F.QUOTES[k].quoted`).
5. Figures breathe (look/lean ±0.02 sine), the camera breathes (`cameraFloat` in film.mjs), plates creep. No frozen frame (smooth.py, seam.mjs FROZEN).
6. Max ~1 hard world change (W) per minute; the sky doors and fly-ins keep the rest continuous.
7. Explainer share per chunk as in `docs/shots.md` §2 (the film: 67.6 %).
