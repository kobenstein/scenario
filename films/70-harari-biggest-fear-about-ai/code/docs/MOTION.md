## 1 · The film in one line
Harari's fear is not killer robots but that we end up like the cow: living inside a system we can no longer understand. Between you and
the systems that decide about your life runs a **chain of trust** with four links (MONEY · CONTRACT · LAW · TRUTH); each one cracks;
Harari's answer is no, the chain need not snap; trust can be tested (three tests) and mended in gold.

## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise: long soft eases (sineIO for glides, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0, a few hard accents on the key word (≤ 2 per 30 s), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph** — one object becomes the next (the link → your card; the beep ring → the light line; the line → the hill → the cow; the ear tag → quote card Q2; the desk screen → the clip plate; the plate → the rule under COLLECTIVE DEMENTIA); (2) **match cut** on a rhyming carrier (same shape, place, size: the chain in the Leitbild ↔ the HUD chain); (3) **carry** — the board view follows an object to the next place (`X.viewPath`); (4) **mask** — a circle grows from the carrier (the card in your hand opens the board, sc01).
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects drop, roll or walk out (away = screen right), fold into a line; never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms (reading order, or outward from the impulse); a group never longer than ~0.8 s.
- **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things; ambient (grid drift, breathing light, figures breathing, the chain's sway) does not count.
- **Camera:** board views move only with a reason (follow a carrier, push into it, pull back for the reveal); the global float (`film.mjs cameraFloat`) keeps every frame alive.
- **Typography:** statements Instrument Serif with a warm glow (`X.big`), labels Inter caps 24–36 px (`X.label`), sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`), ≤ 6 words + one number. Nothing important below y 880.

## 5 · The CHAIN and its HUD
`F.chainLink(ctx, t, x, y, L, ang, { lit, crack, mend, hair, col, mould })` draws one link; `F.chain(ctx, t, pts, …)` a chain along a path (`F.catenary`). `F.chainState(T, t)` gives the state at t from the `F.CHAIN` phrases (on 'the one you would swear is safe'; MONEY/CONTRACT/LAW/TRUTH at their chunk phrases; cracks at 'So the first link holds', 'this is how a link cracks', 'Rules may soon be written faster', 'Then we follow the machine'; point 'Four links. Four cracks'; mend 'And trust can be tested'). `F.chainHud(ctx, t, T)` = the small chain top right (`F.HUD`, from 2.23), with "LINK n · NAME" while a link is examined; it hides itself in clip windows and during anchor 3. Never set a crack or a mend by hand.
