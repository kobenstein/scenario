## 1 · Hook: The Line That Aimed at Friday (0:00)

[Thumbnail-Motiv: rote Tuschekurve rast auf das Kalenderblatt FRI 13 NOV 2026 zu; harter Start]
The singularity was predicted for this Friday. November thirteenth, twenty twenty-six.

[Karte SCIENCE · 4 NOV 1960 mit dem Originaltitel (das Wort „Doomsday“ wird nie gesprochen); die Formel zieht sich als rote Linie bis zum Kalenderblatt]
Not by a prophet. By a formula, in the journal Science, in nineteen sixty. It said the number of people on Earth would shoot up to infinity. Right about now.

[Die reale Linie (Anthrazit) läuft knapp über der roten; auf ihr ein Punkt YOU]
Sounds crazy. But for decades, the world ran right on the formula's schedule. Even ahead of it. If you were born before the mid-nineties, you were born ahead of schedule.

[Die Anthrazit-Linie knickt ab, die rote schießt allein senkrecht hoch]
Then the line bent. The formula never allowed that.

[Daneben steigt eine zweite Kurve steil an: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE]
Now a new line is shooting up, faster than we ever did. This time it isn't people. It's artificial intelligence.

[Rätselkarte WHAT BENT THE FIRST LINE? heftet sich an die erste Linie]
So what bent the first line? Whatever it was, it could bend the second one, too.

[Krieg und Hunger werden durchgestrichen; ein Stammbaum, der nach unten schmaler wird]
Not war. Not famine. Something you can see in your own family tree.

## 2 · The Loop (0:51)

[Kreis aus Pfeilen PEOPLE → INVENTORS → PROGRESS → PEOPLE, dreht sich immer schneller]
Behind that infinity is a loop, and it runs right through your family tree. More people means more inventors. More inventors means more progress: more food, better medicine. More progress means more people. And every round goes faster.

[Derselbe Kreis, die Figuren werden zu abstrakten Maschinen, die Maschinen bauen (keine Roboter); Etikett SINGULARITY]
Today, that same loop is what people mean by the singularity in artificial intelligence. Only now, the inventors are machines that build better machines.

[Im Menschen-Kreis reißt der Pfeil PROGRESS → PEOPLE; kurze Szenen ohne Flaggen: Kind beim Arzt, Mädchen mit Schulheft, Paar mit Kalender; Karte „≈ 5 → ≈ 2 kids per woman“]
But the human loop broke at one link: progress stopped turning into more people. Once most kids survived, parents didn't need to have so many. Girls stayed in school. Couples could plan their families. Worldwide, the average woman went from about five kids to about two.

[Die Rätselkarte bekommt die Antwort MORE CHOICES und wandert an die zweite Linie: COULD IT BEND THIS ONE?]
That's what bent the first line: families got more choices.

[Der Maschinen-Kreis ohne Stammbaum dreht weiter; neben der KI-Linie ein Kalenderblatt „FRI ?“]
Machines don't have a family tree. Nothing in their loop chooses to slow down. Is their line headed for a Friday of its own?

## 3 · The Stopwatch (1:48)

[Fachkraft am Schreibtisch, darüber eine Stoppuhr; daneben dieselbe Aufgabe auf einem Bildschirm]
To see where the machine line is headed, independent researchers keep a stopwatch on it.

[Aufgabenkarte, Stoppuhr läuft; Balken „longest task done alone, at least half the time“]
They take real tasks, like a piece of programming work. They time how long a skilled person needs. Then they find the biggest task the machine can finish on its own, at least half the time.

[Der Balken verdoppelt sich in Sprüngen; eine Sanduhr ONE WORKING DAY]
Every few months, that size doubles. By now, the best systems can handle tasks that take an expert more than a full working day.

[Gleichmäßige Treppe ohne Ende; daneben die Kurve von 1960, deren Stufen immer schmaler werden, bis sie an einer senkrechten Wand mit Datum endet]
Doubling like that is fast. But a steady doubling never hits infinity. It just keeps climbing. The formula from nineteen sixty is different. Each doubling comes faster than the last, until the gaps shrink to nothing. That's what aims it at a date.

[Die Kurve von 1960 legt sich als Geist über die KI-Linie; an ihrem Ende ein Fragezeichen statt eines Datums]
Newer measurements suggest the machine line's doublings already come faster than they used to. If they keep getting faster, the machine line takes the old shape. The shape that aims at a date. Some forecasts assume exactly that.

## 4 · A Birthday · Atempause (2:50)

[Der Kalender zoomt von „late 2026“ auf den 13. November, rot umkreist; die Musik wird leichter]
That shape is how the formula landed on this Friday. Almost. The math only said late twenty twenty-six. The thirteenth came from somewhere else.

[Karteikarte BORN · 13 NOV 1911 · VIENNA; Überfahrt nach Amerika; Campus in Illinois; eine Kerze mit 115]
It's a birthday. The man behind the formula, Heinz von Foerster, was born in Vienna on November thirteenth, nineteen eleven. Later, he moved to America and worked at the University of Illinois. Reportedly, the date in the paper's title was his joke. On Friday, he would be a hundred and fifteen.

[Die Kerzenflamme zwinkert; Titelkarte SCIENCE · 1962 · „Projections versus Forecasts“]
The day was a wink. The math was not. But two years later, the authors called their line a projection, not a forecast.

[Navi am Armaturenbrett: ARRIVAL 6:12 PM; der Fahrer biegt ab, die Ankunftszeit springt um]
A projection is like the arrival time on your GPS. It's only right if you keep driving the same way.

[Die KI-Linie als Route im Navi, Ziel „FRI ?“; die Rätselkarte COULD IT BEND THIS ONE? hängt daran]
The machine line is a projection, too. It shows where we're headed if nothing changes. So who gets to change the route?

## 5 · See the Line · Akt 2 (3:46)

[Windschutzscheibe im Nebel, der Nebel lichtet sich; Namenskarte HEINZ VON FOERSTER · CYBERNETICS]
You can only change a route you can see. Nobody steers with their eyes closed. That idea is the heart of von Foerster's own field. It's called cybernetics, the science of steering.

[Zählbögen aus vielen Ländern stapeln sich zur ersten Linie]
The first line was easy to see, because countries counted their people, again and again.

[Die Stoppuhr wird zur öffentlichen Grafik im Browser; die Schlagzeilen HYPE und PANIC verblassen vor der Kurve]
The machine line is visible because of that stopwatch. Independent researchers measure it, and they share what they find in public. You can look it up yourself. That matters more than any headline. With real numbers, you don't have to guess between hype and panic.

[Ein Kind wächst über ein zu kurzes Lineal hinaus]
And the researchers are honest about a problem. For the best systems, they're running out of tasks that are hard enough. It's like measuring a growing kid with a ruler that's too short.

[Viele Messlatten entlang der Kurve, daneben eine Wetterkarte]
So the measuring has to grow as fast as the line. Out in the open. As easy to check as tomorrow's weather.

[Die erste Linie biegt sich unter Millionen winziger Striche; an der KI-Linie die Karte MORE CHOICES FOR WHOM?]
But seeing a line doesn't bend it. The first one bent when more choices reached billions of families. Who will these machines give more choices to? Everyone, or just a few?

## 6 · Many Hands (4:58)

[Die Sanduhr ONE WORKING DAY rinnt in offene Hände: ein Vertrag, eine Geschäftsidee auf einer Serviette]
The answer comes down to who gets help from these machines. Picture that full working day of expert help, aimed at your own problems. The contract you couldn't afford a lawyer for. The business idea you shelved years ago.

[Ein Expertenbüro hinter einer Samtkordel; die Kordel fällt, die Tür steht offen]
For most of history, help like that was rare and expensive. Only a lucky few had it on call. Now it could be on call for anyone.

[Anonyme Kommentarkarte mit dem ganzen Satz „I don't see that ideal scenario where the public reaps the benefits…“; dann Lichtpunkte über der ganzen Weltkarte]
In comments about this Friday, the biggest worry isn't speed. It's who gets the benefits. This time, the help has to reach billions of people, not just the few who build it.

[Kurzer Halt: Karte WHO DECIDES?]
But who decides where that help goes?

[Wenige Hände an einem großen Steuerrad in einem stillen Raum, keine Bösewichte; dahinter acht Milliarden Lichtpunkte]
Right now, a few people make the big calls. The ones who build these systems, fund them, and write their rules. That's not a villain story. It's a heavy load for very few. And no small group, however well-meaning, can know what eight billion people need.

[Millionen winziger Pfeile in Familienküchen drehen sich nacheinander in dieselbe Richtung; die erste Linie biegt sich]
The first line bent without one shared plan, through billions of family decisions.

[Küchentisch mit Handy und Schul-Laptop, eine Hand legt eine App weg; dann eine Wahlkabine]
The machine line can bend the same way. No family decides how fast these machines learn. But billions of us help decide what they're for. Every day, in what we use and what we refuse. And at the ballot box.

[Ein Schaufenster voller glänzender neuer Geräte; Karte BIGGER OR SMALLER?]
Still, choosing is hard when everything new looks shiny. How can you tell whether something new makes your life bigger, or smaller?

## 7 · The Birthday Rule · Finale (6:28)

[Die Kerze mit 115 auf dem Kalenderblatt]
To tell bigger from smaller, the man behind this Friday left a test.

[Handgeschriebene Karte 1973, die Schrift entsteht Wort für Wort]
In nineteen seventy-three, he put it in a single line. "Act always so as to increase the number of choices."

[OPTIONS wird durchgestrichen, CHOICES bleibt]
And he was picky about that last word. Not options. Choices.

[Ein Bildschirm voller Knöpfe verblasst; eine Hand trifft eine eigene Entscheidung]
A screen full of buttons gives you options. A choice is a decision that's truly yours.

[Prüfkarte MORE DECISIONS OF YOUR OWN, NOT FEWER; eine Hand hält sie an ein Handy, ein Formular, eine Wahlurne; ein Daumen sagt Nein]
Hold his rule up to anything new. After it arrives, people should make more of their own decisions, not fewer. Including the decision to say no.

[Ein Kind lernt mit einem Tutor auf dem Tablet (Haken); ein Formular füllt sich von selbst aus (Kreuz)]
A tutor that lets any kid learn whatever they want passes the test. A system that quietly decides for you fails it.

[Die KI-Linie, an ihr viele Hände; Menschen im Mittelpunkt]
The test isn't how smart these machines get. It's how many choices they leave in human hands.

[Die erste Linie mit dem YOU-Punkt im Knick; an der KI-Linie setzt eine Hand den nächsten Knick an]
That's exactly how the first line bent, quietly, inside your lifetime. The next bend, you can see coming.
