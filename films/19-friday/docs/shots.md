## 1 · How to read this
- **One chunk = one plan sequence** on THE SHEET in the night (`MOTION.md` §2). A row is a shot inside the sequence, not a cut.
- **Layer:** `[EXPL]` = light and ink on the sheet (the chart, cards, the stopwatch, stairs); `[SCENE]` = layers lifted off the sheet
  (you, silhouettes, rooms, the car); `[SCENE>EXPL]` / `[EXPL>SCENE]` = one turns into the other (LIFT-OFF / LAY-DOWN); `[CLIP]` = ORIGINAL FOOTAGE in its frame.
- **Explainer share (target 60–70 %):** weighted over the animated runtime (film minus clips): EXPL = 1, mixed = ½, SCENE = 0; the end
  screen counts as mixed. The generator prints it per chunk and for the film (below the chunk table).
- **Anchors:** the bold phrases. An accent lands on its word (±2 frames); camera keys sit on words.
- **Moves (≤ 2):** the hero motion ① and at most one support motion ②. Everything else is ambient.
- **Transition:** "plan sequence" = the next shot continues in the same move; otherwise carrier · screen position · direction.
  **SEAM** rows hand over to another builder through a plate (`lib/f19_seams.mjs`).
- **★** = the one wow moment of the chunk (c02 has none: it is the explainer chunk with C1).
- One picture per sentence, never a still image, readable without sound. No generated real people: von Foerster only as silhouette,
  signature or his real clip.
- **Fact-sensitive anchors** (the fact check may still move words): `more than a full working day`, `about five kids to about two`,
  `Reportedly`, `nineteen seventy-three`, `eight billion`. Anchor on a neighbouring stable phrase or guard with `T.gHas`.
