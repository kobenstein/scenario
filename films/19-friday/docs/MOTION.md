## 2 · ONE world: THE SHEET in the night
The channel look stays: a calm, dark indigo space, one gold light, one tiny you in the yellow raincoat. What is this film's own is the
**motif**: the page of *Science*, 4 November 1960, on which von Foerster, Mora and Amiot drew humanity's line to infinity.
- **THE SHEET** is a giant map of 1960 graph paper drawn in **blue light on indigo** (`sheet()`), lying in the night under **one gold
  lamp** (a soft gold pool, `lamp`, `lampR`). It is the explainer surface of the whole film: every chart is drawn **on it**, never in a panel.
- **EXPL = light on the sheet.** The **first line** (people) is **gold ink** with a wet head (`ink()`); print is cream type; the
  **second line** (the machine) is a line of **cool cyan light** (`lightLine()`) that spills onto the paper (`sheet({ cool })`).
- **SCENE = layers lifted off the sheet.** Indigo silhouettes with blue-lit edges, gold-lit windows and holes, and you, rise from the
  sheet (`lifted(ctx, c, lift, fn)`, real parallax, a soft shadow on the sheet). They lay down again into the next diagram.
- **Leitbild only as anchor:** the full 1960 page (`chart1960`) appears at the anchors — the hook, the question (2.01), the asymptote
  (6.01/6.03), the end (11.07). Elsewhere you work in other regions of the sheet (it is 7400 × 4900 design px).
- Sheet coordinates (design px at the home camera): the 1960 chart lives at x 160 (1800) … 1633 (2026), y 960 (0 bn) upward;
  the AI field at x 1960 … 2700; the slip at (1640, −1000). Lifted layers stand nearer by `lift · LIFT` (240).

## 5 · Calm rules (measurable)
- **World changes:** ≤ ~1 hard world change per minute. Everything else is plan sequence on the sheet, LIFT-OFF/LAY-DOWN, or a push into an object.
- **Motion budget:** at most 1–2 moving main elements at once, the camera counts. Ambient life (`camDrift`, swings, the lamp breathing) does not.
- **Camera keys sit on words** (`camAt(t, K, ZS)` with keys from `T.G`); always pass `ref = ZS`. Cameras via `aim(ZS, u, v, s)`.
- **One motif per sentence. Nothing from nowhere:** ink is drawn by its head, cards drop in on their pins, labels rise from their baseline
  (`label(…, u)`), layers lift off the sheet. Never fade in half-transparent.
- **Never a still image > 2 s; hold 1.5–3 s after each accent.** Text holds while read (≥ 1.5 s, ≥ characters/12 s; quote cards ≥ 4 s).
- **Measure:** `python3 tools/calm.py <mp4> --scale 1280:720` (cuts ≤ 0.9/min, mafd median ≤ ~0.45, **luma 35–65** for the night sheet),
  `python3 tools/smooth.py <mp4> <start>` (**0 LURCH/POP**), `SECS=cNN,cMM node tools/seam.mjs --at T0` at every chunk seam.
