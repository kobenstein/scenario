# Film 55 · "The Best AI Is Locked Away" · motion bible (lead, 2026-11-17)

Binding for every chunk. Shots: `docs/shots.md` (generated by `tools/shots55.py`). Builders: `docs/BRIEF-builders.md`. Reference: `scenes/sc01.mjs` (the hook, built by the lead) and `<ROOT>bild/c01_kontakt.jpg`, `<ROOT>bild/kit55_kontakt.jpg`. Text: **vo-v4.txt is final** (ROOT).

## 1 · Intent
- **Mood:** calm, curious, fair. The best AI is not hidden, it is *announced*; what is locked is the access and some of the evidence. Every company figure says so on screen.
- **Explainer share 60–70 %** (shots.md §1 computes it: EXPL/CARD 1 · MIX .5 · LINE .3 · SCENE 0). The world explains: marks, groups, the door, the tape, the sheets.
- **One idea per sentence.** At most 1–2 moving elements at once (the camera counts as one).

## 2 · The world: THE LINE (one continuous world, thumbnail A)
- A night plain under stars, the city small on the horizon at the left. A colossal wall block on the right with **ONE colossal closed door** far away (z 14800). **Gold leaks** around its single leaf (top and lock side; no handle) and lies on the floor as a warm pool toward you.
- **The line:** 46 people from the threshold back to you, standing on **five floor marks** painted on the floor: 1 THE LAB · 2 GOVERNMENT · 3 BIG COMPANIES · 4 DEFENDERS · 5 YOU. Groups by kind (`lab` coats, `gov` badges, `suit`, `tools` cases, `plain`). **You** = the yellow coat (E.du) on mark 5. Behind you, **everyone else** curves away to the horizon (`S.TAIL`, revealed in c10).
- A small **side door** right of the big one (cream practical light): defenders (c07) and testers (c12) are let in there.
- **Portals, not cuts:** the kitchen opens out of your phone screen; glimpses inside open out of the door gap; the night sky out of the kitchen window. Each closes back into its thing (`F.portal`).
- **The proof tape** (through-line): a paper strip of proof rows, each gets a small **cyan tick**. Out of the gap in the hook ("Not sums. Proofs."), Fermat in c02, back in the big answer (c13).
- **Calendar sheets** are the only time device. **Cores** (faceted gold rhombs) are the machines: the same core behind the door and in your phone; `dim` = an older matte machine. **Clipboard** = the inspector (WHO CHECKED IT?). **Quote cards** replace all footage.
- The camera is a **real pinhole camera** (`S.proj`, `S.look`, `S.camPath`): sx = W/2 + (X − c.x)·s, sy = hz + (EYE − Y − c.y)·s, s = f/(Z − c.z). Each camera has its own horizon `hz` (default 830, low like thumbnail A); `S.look` raises it by itself when a shot needs the floor higher. Fly with `S.camPath` (log distance; safe for deep flights door ↔ you).

## 4 · Motion DNA
- Long glides of 2–4 s along and over the line; soft accents on the key words (a mark lights, a tick draws, a sheet falls, a stamp lands).
- **Nothing stands still:** camera drift (`S.drift`, ~13 px at any depth), the leak breathes, dust in the gap, people breathe, stars twinkle, and **the paper** (`F.paper`, drawn by the world, the kitchen and the room) slides a fine print grain under everything. Never over people or text.
- The wow moment of each chunk is marked ★ in shots.md. One per chunk.

## 6 · Calm rules (all chunks)
1. One motif per sentence. The picture changes with the sentence, not faster.
2. Scenes and explainers alternate. No two explainers of the same kind in a row.
3. At most **1–2 moving elements** at a time. The rest only breathes.
4. One continuous take per chunk; inner chunk boundaries within a group are planned match frames (only cuts in film.mjs, no blends).
5. Nothing appears from nowhere: it slides out of the gap, rises from a mark, falls as a sheet, grows out of a screen, is stamped.
6. Text: ≤ 6 words and at most one number per statement, readable at 390 px width, on screen ≥ 1.2 s.
7. **Subtitles are burned in at the bottom: no important text below y 880.** Source lines sit at y ≤ 880 (`F.source`, default y 870).
8. **No QR overlay, no quiet corner, no QR window** (rule 16.11.2026).
9. Numbers per chunk: calm.py (0 cuts, luma 40–60, static share as low as possible, target < 10 %; mafd median ≈ 0.6–0.8 at 720p), smooth.py 0 LURCH / 0 POP except at a planned seam frame.

## 9 · Gates (motion-director-gates)
1. **Plan:** this file + shots.md before code.
2. **Space:** a real perspective camera through one world (`S.camPath`), flat shapes in 3D.
3. **Carrier camera:** the camera follows the phone light, the gap, the tape, the marks lighting one after another.
4. **Causality:** marks light when the camera passes them; ticks follow the written rows; a sheet falls on its number.
5. **5 s test:** c01 is the reference.
6. **Review frames** every 0.33–1 s plus every transition, then a contact sheet of the whole chunk.
7. **The voice drives timing:** phrase anchors only (`T.G`, `T.GE`, `T.clip`), never seconds.
8. **Honest self-grade** in every report.
