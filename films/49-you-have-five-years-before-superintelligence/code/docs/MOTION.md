## 1 · Intent
A viewer of 50 or 60 should feel: "five years is a staircase I can climb, one step a year, and the top is mine to decide." Not fear of a
machine: a calm night, a warm light at the top, a small figure in a yellow raincoat who climbs. Every number sits ON the stairs, so a TV
viewer always knows where in the countdown we are.

## 3 · Motion DNA (owner rules, binding)
1. **One world, the camera travels instead of cutting.** 0 hard cuts and 0 world changes in the film. Slow camera (`F.camPath` through
   phrase keys, `E.camDrift` on top). Max 1–2 moving elements at a time: when a card or a figure moves, the camera only creeps.
2. **A carrier at every transition** (each scene file has a header table): the door light, the gold wave, the light path, the string
   lights, the place card, a paper card that a clip grows out of, a step that lights.
3. **Nothing from nowhere, no ghost fades.** Stage sets pop up out of the tread; date cards unfold from the step edge (`F.tentCard`);
   text rises behind a mask (`F.caption`); clips grow out of a paper card or a list and go back into it.
4. **Never still.** Camera drift, `F.float`, breathing door, dust, string lights flicker. Plates keep CREEP 5 %/s and TRUCK 45 px/s; the
   chunk next to a plate continues that speed (c01 runs the plate creep backwards for its last 1.5 s).
5. **Speed:** big area changes ≥ 1.8 s sineIO (smooth.py LURCH > 12); hits (a step lights, a card lands) ≤ .3 s on the word, then ≥ .8 s
   calm.
6. **One ★ per chunk** (shots.md). Every picture illustrates its sentence.
7. **Explainer share 60–70 %** (shots.md §1: now 61.3 %). Explainers stand on the stairs (date cards, numbered risers, bars on a tread)
   or float over `F.veil` while the world glides behind.
8. **Clips** (C13, C5, C16 portrait shorts centred on night blue; C2 landscape): `F.clipPlate`. Until the footage arrives the plate shows
   ORIGINAL FOOTAGE · NAME on dark ground; `<ROOT>clips/Cn.mp4` is picked up automatically (`tools/f49_clips.py clips`, also run by
   `render`). Slots will shift once the real clips are in: anchor on `T.clip(id)`, never on seconds.
