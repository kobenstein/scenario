## Beschreibung
In 1965 Gordon Moore drew a line through a handful of dots. It's been sixty years. Now everyone says AI is at "the turning point". But on a true exponential the bend isn't in the curve, it's in where you cut the chart. So what actually tells you something? And how do we act early enough?
