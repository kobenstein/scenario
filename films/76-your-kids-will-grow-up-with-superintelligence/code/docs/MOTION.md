## 1 · The film in one line
Your child's question at the kitchen table ("What should I be when I grow up?") has no safe answer any more; we walk the child up five
ages (0 · 6 · 12 · 18 · 30) in one scenario, and what is left is not a job but a kind of person: you can't know *what*, you can help with *who*.

## 3 · Carrier: THE PAPER PLANE
The child's question, written in the notebook at the kitchen table, is folded into a paper plane in h5. From then on (and already in h1
as foreshadowing, leaving the child's hand at the gate) every boundary is the plane: it **leaves shot A at A.out** (the last element of A
sits there), flies a lifted arc for 0.55 s (`FLY`) with bank and a dotted pencil trail, **lands at B.in**, opens, and B unfolds as a disc
with a soft edge and a thin paper rim (0.7 s, expo-out, `REV`). The first element of B is born exactly at B.in. `tr: 'match'` = B continues
A's last frame by itself (no plane). In s6/e1 the plane flies into the gate. Implemented once in `film.mjs` (`Seq.frame`, `planePath`);
shots only have to honour `in` / `out` from plan.mjs.

## 5 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise (premium/editorial cell): sineIO for glides, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits, overshoot ≈ 0; ≤ 2 hard accents per 30 s, then ≥ 0.8 s calm. Kids' world = a little softer (a short settle bob on landings), never toy-like.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s). Big area changes ≥ 1.6 s.
- **Transitions:** only the plane, a match, or a morph inside a shot (the page folds into the plane, the cards tip flat, figures dim, a column grows into a label). Never a fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms by reading order or outward from S.in; a group ≤ 0.8 s.
- **Camera:** `K.shotCam` (slow push per shot, with a reason) + the global float (`cameraFloat`); kitchen push runs across h4 + h5 (`kitCam`) so the match seam is invisible.
- **Typography:** statements Instrument Serif with warm glow (`K.big`, `E.glowText`), labels Inter caps 24–36 px (`K.cap`), sources 22 px at y 880 (`K.src`), ≤ 6 words + one number; nothing important below y 880; text held ≥ 1.5 s.
- **Figures:** you and your child = the yellow coat (`E.du`, `K.kid`), faceless; everyone else indigo (`K.folk`, `K.figRow`). The child grows by age (`K.KIDS` head scales).
