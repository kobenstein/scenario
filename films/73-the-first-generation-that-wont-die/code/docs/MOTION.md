## 1 · The film in one line
Every generation before us was given the answer; we are the first to be asked the question. Two ways out of death (repair the body ·
copy the mind), then a world without death (marriage, work, inheritance, power), then "do we want this?" and the good version.
The picture language follows that: **a life is a line**. The dash on a headstone ("1985 –") is pulled out of the stone and becomes
**THE LIFELINE**, the stage on which the film explains everything. **THE ANSWER** (NO ·—●—· YES) returns every time the question does.
Four times the film steps back to **THE STONE** (thumbnail A).

## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise: long soft eases (sineIO for glides, quartIO for morphs, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0; ≤ 2 hard accents per 30 s (the crack tearing open on "won't" is one), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph** — an object becomes the next (stone → drawing → dash → line; card → answer; ring → computer; cube → data wall; dots → people); (2) **fly-through** — the camera dives into a screen or a window and the next picture lives inside (H.13, 2.02, 4.01); (3) **grow-out** — a thing rises out of the line and sinks back into it (head, body, computer, rings, ledge). A hard cut only where shots.md has **W**, and then over a rhyming shape.
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects sink into the line, lift off, or roll out right; never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms by reading order or outward from the impulse; a group never longer than ~0.8 s (dot fields: `F.dots` staggers by itself).
- **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things; ambient (stars, breathing light, figures breathing, the bead's tremble) does not count.
- **Camera:** the view (`X.viewPath`, monotone, log scale) moves only with a reason: follow you or the line's head, push into an object, pull back for a reveal (1.13 the four centuries, M.11 the town). `S.breathe(t)` + the global float keep every frame alive.
- **Typography:** title Inter Black condensed (`F.title`, frame 0 only); statements Instrument Serif with a warm glow (`X.big`); labels Inter caps 24–36 px (`X.label`); sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`); ≤ 6 words + one number; "AI" on screen, never "A.I.". Nothing important below y 880.

## 4 · Colour code (meaning, never violate)
GOLD = life, health, what holds; the ONE hero light per frame (the crack, the lifeline, a lit joint, a gold number) · CREAM = text,
paper, drawings, open questions, an end tick · COOL (pale blue) = machines, AI, computers, the copy (dashed when it is the copy of you) ·
AGE (grey-violet) = decline, old age · LAMP (warm cream) = people's windows · the yellow coat = only you. Never red. The good version
(c11 from "It doesn't have to go") may warm the night towards dawn (scenario-motion: reserved for the solution part).

## 5 · THE ANSWER (the motor)
`F.answer(ctx, t, x, y, w, p, o)`: a short piece of lifeline with NO at the left end, YES at the right end and a gold bead (p −1 … 1).
It is not a switch or a button: nobody presses it; the bead drifts where the arguments push it, it trembles while the question is open.
It appears ONLY when the question returns (H.07–H.09, 2.25–2.26 cool, 3.01, 3.08, M.05–M.07, 4.19, 5.23–5.24, 6.30, E.11 plain), never as
a HUD. Weights hang under its ends (3.08, M.07, 5.24) — draw them as small dark/gold drops on a thread, in your chunk.
