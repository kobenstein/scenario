promise, half condition: the machine that invents everything else is the last thing we need to invent, PROVIDED we keep it under control;

## 3 · Motion DNA (calm × sharp, editorial)
| dial | choice |
|---|---|
| Energy | calm, 2–4 s glides against few hard accents (≤ 2 per 30 s, ≤ 3 frames), ≥ 0.8 s hold after an accent |
| Easing | entrances `expoOut`/`rise`/`F.backOut` (tiny), exits `expoIn`, moves `sineIO`, deliberate transitions `quartIO`; never linear except rotation/creep/flaps |
| Overshoot | ~0. Weight from timing: heavy things start slow and land with a 2–5 % squash (80 YEARS drums, the band lands) |
| Type | Inter Black for thumbnail words only · Instrument Serif for numbers, quotes, the band · Inter 600 caps labels ≥ 22 px · split-flap mono for board rows (`F.flapText`) |
| Text entry | words rise behind their own line mask on the spoken word (±2 frames), or flap in on the board; exits sink behind the mask or leave with their carrier |
| Colour | night indigo ground; **GOLD** = the invention light (begun, real, the promise); **CREAM** = questions, wishes, outlines, the open 4th line, the condition; **COOL** = the machine's own light (the ring); **yellow coat = only you**; other people indigo. FILLED = real/checked, DASHED = imagined/forecast. Never red |
| Camera | `cameraFloat` breathes over everything; each motif has its own `F.look`; carrier-following moves via `F.lookPath(t, K)` keyed on phrases; the camera never leads the carrier |
| Life | figures breathe and look, the ring rotates/thinks, flaps settle, plates breathe (`S.breathe`) and creep (2 %/s) |
| Finish | `F.PRINT77` (grain on threes, vignette); grain never over type or the figure |
| Signature | **the empty last row**: a glowing slot at the bottom of a list; A.G.I. is typed into it, it stretches into Good's band, row 4 stays open |

## 4 · Carriers (transition vocabulary; no crossfades, no wipes)
1. **Shape becomes shape:** a board row → the bulb; icons → rows; the gold row → the dot sheet; the card → the empty row → the band; a word lifts off a card and flaps into a row.
2. **Camera follows the carrier** (`F.lookPath`): the row falls, the camera tilts after it (c01).
3. **Iris** (`F.iris`) and **portal** (`F.portal`, ends exactly on B's identity frame).
4. **Line draws itself** (`F.outline`, `F.trailCream`, `F.goldSpan`): dashed = imagined, gold = real.
5. **Clip plate** grows out of an object (a slot, the furnace window, the package) and returns into it.
6. **Seam plates** between builder groups (`lib/f77_seams.mjs`): both sides draw the same plate ±0.5 s with the same creep.

c01 transition map (built, `scenes/sc01.mjs`): thumbnail (board, ring, you, FINAL HUMAN INVENTION with a gold rule under each spoken word)
→ the LIGHT BULB row flips loose and falls; the camera tilts down after it into **your room** → bulb, phone, screen light on their words →
they lift as cream line icons and fly to three inventors' benches whose lamps light (INVENTED BY A PERSON) → the icons drop as rows into a
column that scrolls ever faster through 26 inventions → it brakes onto the empty last row, A.G.I. flaps in gold and unfolds to ARTIFICIAL
GENERAL INTELLIGENCE → a head in profile, its light copied into the ring (WHAT A HUMAN MIND DOES) → a stylus of light lifts off the ring →
**★ it starts a second column that outraces the human one and outgrows the frame** (IT INVENTS FOR US / FASTER THAN WE EVER COULD) → you walk in,
SO WHAT WILL IT INVENT FOR YOU?, dashed rows → the first row fills gold (ALREADY BEGUN) → it unfolds into the Erdős dot sheet (equal-distance
pairs light) → drums roll to 80 YEARS + caption → Gowers card → the card folds into the empty row → the band, typed: PROMISE gold /
CONDITION under a dashed cover / UP TO US; you walk under it → `S.plateBand` → B1.
