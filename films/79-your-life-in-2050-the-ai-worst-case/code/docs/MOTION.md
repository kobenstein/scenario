# Film 79 · "Your Life in 2050: The AI Worst Case" · motion bible · as of 2026-11-25

The mirror of #74 ("Your Life in 2050 If We Get AI Right", mastered). Same street, same stations, same day clock, same alarm-clock bracket,
everything turned the wrong way. Release Sun 6.12.

Skills (lead and every builder, before any code): **scenario-motion** (channel style, wins on conflict), **world-class-motion-design**,
**fluid-motion-design**, **motion-director-gates**; **dataviz** for every number graphic. No ad skills. Style: `stil/STIL-editorial-nacht.md`
(night-blue ground, flat editorial shapes, grain as texture, the yellow coat = you). Rules: REGELN §13 (branch `claude/regeln-bau`):
one picture per sentence, **explainer share 60–70 %**, many own motifs (no "one big world with zooms"), the benchmark is a beautiful
motion-design film; the leitbild is a thought, the street is only an anchor. End screen: 15 s calm closing picture with free areas left
and right for the YouTube end cards, music and picture keep running.
Shot list: `docs/shots.md` (one row per sentence, binding, generated by `tools/shots79.py`). Builders: `docs/BRIEF-builders.md`.

## 1 · Intent
**Leitgedanke:** "Nothing in this street was destroyed. We just stopped being needed."
A walk through YOUR street on one Monday in 2050, from waking up to the bench at night. Nothing is broken, nobody is cruel, no war, no
robot army, no villains. **Menacing through calm**: everything is clean, trimmed, quiet and works perfectly, and none of it needs you.
The threat is never an attack; it is the machine's light that never goes out (the tower, the data centre seam, the screens) and the human
lights that go out one by one (the kitchen on schedule, the school, the square).

Every place the script names is a real **2.5D scene** (flat shapes on cards at true depth, perspective camera `cam3`/`card`, parallax,
one hero light, living figures). Every number, mechanism and "back in the twenties" is a graphic: **MIX** (on the scene: ink ghosts,
paper cards, counters, tags) or **EXPL** (its own explainer stage on the night ground, `lib/f79_expl.mjs`). Graphics carry understanding,
scenes carry feeling (§13.3).

**Numbers (plan):** explainer share target **≥ 62 % for the film** (c01 hook 36 % by design, it carries feeling; c02–c09 must sit at 65–75 %;
c10 ≈ 50 %). Recomputed by `tools/shots79.py` on every timing file; outside 60–70 % → rebuild shots, not keep building.

## 5 · Light rules (bindend)
1. The machine's light (carmine) **never goes out and never flickers out**: it only breathes. A flicker is allowed only as anticipation before a beat (`wake`).
2. Human light goes out on screen at most three times in the film, each as one of the ≤ 3-frame accents: the kitchen (schedule), the school windows, the last lamp at home before the bench. Never more (otherwise it becomes a horror device).
3. The carmine is never the brightest thing in a frame where you or the grandson are the subject: your coat and the brass clock stay the warmest, and in a frame with you the tower band is at most as bright as your coat's rim (scenario-motion: no two heroes of equal strength).
4. Every threatening picture has a visible counterpart in the same section (scenario-motion §8): the clock in the boy's hands, a lit human window, the way-out pass.
5. Day clock = sky: never a warm sun. Dawn and noon read as cold daylight under a night-blue ground, evening and night are night.

## 7 · Carriers (no crossfades, no wipes, no hard cuts)
1. **Portal** (`K.portal`): push through a window/door/frame/screen of A, B lives inside it (the front door, the kitchen window, the boy's screen, the phone).
2. **Iris** (`K.iris`): a round thing of A (the 2050 disc, the dust ring, the clock face, the ring, a window chime point, the phone's notification dot) grows and B opens inside it.
3. **Lens sweep** (`K.sweep`): a near layer (a door post, a hedge, a lamp post, the fence bar) passes the lens on a lateral truck; the truck continues in B.
4. **Paper card**: unfolds out of an object (MIX, the past), the scene stays alive behind it, folds back into the object. Clips grow out of a paper card (the past) and fold back into it.
5. **Graphic out of the scene**: an EXPL builds out of an object of the scene (the tower band becomes the bar of a chart, the phone's line becomes the ALLOWANCE line, the fence bars become the labour-share bar) and falls back into the next scene.
6. **Same place, new view** only when the shot list says `no (beat)`.
The HUD stays across every seam. Every boundary has its carrier noted in `docs/shots.md`.
