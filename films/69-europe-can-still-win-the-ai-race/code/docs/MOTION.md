## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise (premium/editorial cell): long soft eases (sineIO for glides, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0, a few hard accents on the key word (≤ 2 per 30 s), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph** — one object becomes the next (the moon → the AI iris; the disc outline → 100 cells; 5 gold cells → the machine's windows; the crowd → one disc → 27 crates; 27 stacks → one sheet; a report cover → a clip plate); (2) **match cut** on a rhyming carrier (same shape, place, size: the moon ↔ the iris); (3) **carry** — an object leaves and uncovers / lands in the next picture (the machine rolls away and uncovers the person; the plan slides in; the drawing lifts off the plan); (4) **pull back / push** into a carrier (the desk becomes one window of a town; push into a kit piece = "Fach n wird groß").
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects drop, roll or walk out (away = screen right), fall away downward; never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms (by reading order, or outward from the impulse); a group never longer than ~0.8 s.
- **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things; ambient (grid drift, breathing light, figures breathing) does not count.
- **Camera:** the sheet view moves only with a reason (follow a carrier, push into it, pull back for the reveal); the global float (`film.mjs cameraFloat`) keeps every frame alive.
- **Typography:** statements in Instrument Serif with a warm glow (`X.big`), labels Inter caps 24–36 px (`X.label`), sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`), ≤ 6 words + one number. Nothing important below y 880.

## 4 · Colour code (meaning, never violate)

Away = screen right; coming back = right → left.

## 5 · The KIT (the plan, five parts)
`F.kitDisc` draws the disc anywhere, `F.kitParts(T, t)` gives the state at t, `F.kitHud` is the small HUD top right (from 1.13, hidden in clip windows; `o.big` = the verdict picture). It changes by itself on the `F.KIT` phrases: being checked = the piece pulses, PART n OF 5 + name; HALFWAY = gold, seated only half (pushed out); ON PAPER = cream paper, dashed edge; OPEN = the slot stays empty, the piece lies beside the disc (or in your hands). Never red, never a cross, never a stamp, never a clock (§9.4: no motifs of earlier films).
