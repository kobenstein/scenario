Generated tables between the SHOTS markers come from `tools/shots23.py` (edit the generator, then `python3 tools/shots23.py --md docs/shots.md`; the text outside the markers stays). Times come from the real VO (`film-erster-tag/2037/audio/timing.json`) as soon as the audio agent writes it; **until then they are PROVISIONAL** (`data/provisional_timing.json`, 145 wpm). The code anchors on the **bold phrases** only, never on the seconds.

## 1 · How to read this
- **One chunk = one plan sequence** in THE STREET IN SECTION (`MOTION.md` §2). A row is a shot inside the sequence, not a cut.
- **Layer:** `[SCENE]` = the 2.5D world (the street, the flats, the figures), `[EXPL]` = an explainer graphic (light on night) grown from a world object, in the sky, in a window space or on the kitchen's light wall; `[SCENE>EXPL]` / `[EXPL>SCENE]` = the shot holds the transformation (counts half); `[CLIP]` = ORIGINAL FOOTAGE on the old screen (`MOTION.md` §5), excluded from the explainer share.
- **Explainer share** = explainer seconds / picture seconds of the spoken film without the clip slots (mixed shots half). **Target 60–70 %** (Film 18: 43 %). The generator prints it per chunk and in total.
- **Anchors:** the bold phrases with their time. An accent lands on its word (±2 frames); camera keys sit on words.
- **Moves (≤ 2):** the hero motion ① and at most one support motion ②. Everything else is ambient.
- **Transition:** every row names the carrier object into the next shot (· position · direction). **SEAM** rows hand over to another builder through a plate (`lib/f23_seams.mjs`); **CLIP IN / CLIP OUT** rows use the old screen (`MOTION.md` §5).
- **★** = the one wow moment of the chunk, exactly one per chunk.
- **Cards E1–E13** (the "Already …" evidence cards) and the **day-clock** are drawn by the HUD (`lib/f23_hud.mjs`, lead) over the scenes; a row only says on which word a card opens. Builders keep the reserved zones free (`MOTION.md` §9).
- One motif per sentence; a number is on screen only while its sentence runs plus the hold.
