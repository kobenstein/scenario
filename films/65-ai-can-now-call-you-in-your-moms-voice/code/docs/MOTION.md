## 1 · Intent
- **Mood:** calm, protective, practical and warm. This is not a horror film about machines. It tells the story of five steps a scam has to pass. A machine can fake four of them, and it cannot fake the fifth. The answer is a few agreements made at the kitchen table on a calm day.
- **Respect for her.** The mother is never shown as foolish. "She was certain" means feeling sure is not the same as being right. Show no mocking pose and no dumb face.
- **Interested senders say so on screen:** THE COMPANY REPORTS ON ITSELF, A LOBBY. Unmeasured things say so too: NEVER RELIABLY MEASURED, NEVER MEASURED, ONE SMALL STUDY · 22 PEOPLE.
- **Explainer share:** 72.6 % (shots.md §1; EXPL/CARD 1 · MIX .5 · WORLD .3 · SCENE 0). The world does the explaining through the voices, the wire, the tags, the notes and the phone.
- **One idea per sentence.** At most 1–2 moving elements at once, and the camera counts as one.

## 2 · The world: THE LINE (one continuous world, `lib/f65_seams.mjs`)
- **The setting:** a sleeping valley town at night (`S.lineWorld`). The layers, from back to front:
  - sky and moon;
  - the far ridge, with the cold glow behind it (`st.source`);
  - mid hills and the river;
  - the town, 46 houses with warm windows (`S.TOWN`, `S.WINDOWS`);
  - **her hill** (left);
  - **your hill** (near right).
- **HER HOUSE** sits far left on her hill (`S.WORLD.house`). It has ONE warm kitchen window. The **kitchen** (`S.kitchen`) opens out of that window as a portal (`F.portal` from `S.winRect(c)`). It contains:
  - the fridge with five note slots (`S.NOTE_SLOTS`);
  - the swaying lamp;
  - the table with the phone;
  - her (`F.mom` with `F.reachArm`: she reaches for the phone and lifts it to her ear);
  - in c04 and c16, you at the table opposite her.
- **THE LINE:** one telephone wire on **five wooden poles** runs from her house down the hill to **THE BANK** at its foot (`S.WORLD.poles`, `S.WIRE`, `S.WORLD.bank`).
  - Each pole carries a small numbered tag **1–5**. These are the five steps the scam has to pass: voice, story, face, clock, money.
  - `st.tags.lit(k)` turns a tag gold once its step is explained. `hi` marks the step being told now.
  - `st.ghosts` adds blue copies beside poles 1–4, the machine's fakes. Pole 5 has none: a copy of pole 5 slides off.
  - `st.wire.gold` / `u` runs gold light down the wire.
- **THE BANK** has a calm, lit counter window with a real teller (`st.bank { win, glow, teller }`). The counter opens as a portal out of `S.bankWinRect(c)` (B3).
- **YOU** stand on the near hill right (`S.WORLD.you`): the yellow coat, hood up, with your phone (`st.you { phone, screen, ring, light, look }`).
- **THE VOICE** is a ribbon of light (`F.voiceWave` along `S.arc3(c, A, B, lift)` in the world, or `F.arcPts` in screen space). Its colour carries the meaning:
  - **GOLD** is a real person's voice.
  - **COPY**, electric blue, is the machine's copy: the same shape, cold.
  - **NEUTRAL** is a voice you can no longer judge by ear.
- **THE GLOW** behind the far ridge (`st.source`, `S.WORLD.source`) is where the copies come from. It is never shown as an orb, an eye or a face; it stays a cold light below a ridge.
- **THE AGREEMENTS** are five gold paper notes on her fridge (`F.note`), in this order:
  1. the word (always covered by a gold bar; the word is never shown);
  2. the question "?";
  3. the house rule FAMOUS FACE + PROFIT = FAKE;
  4. the stop rule HURRY + SECRET + MONEY = STOP;
  5. the name at the bank, TRUSTED CONTACT.

  `empty` draws the dashed place where a note will go.
- **THE PHONE** (`F.phone`) is the film's object. Its screens:
  - `call` rings with your picture (`F.avatar`, the yellow hood on blue);
  - `timer` shows the call timer;
  - `video` is the fake video call, its seal a little crooked;
  - `contacts` has your name lit;
  - `wave`;
  - `dark`.

  Every clip grows out of a screen in the world.
- **The camera** is a real pinhole camera (`S.proj`, `S.look`, `S.camPath`, `S.drift`):
  - Projection: sx = W/2 + (X − c.x)·s, sy = hz + (EYE − Y − c.y)·s, s = f/(Z − c.z), with EYE 160 and F0 1700.
  - `S.look(X, Y, Z, mag, sx, sy, hz)` puts a world point at a screen point (mag 1 means the point at its natural size from the reference distance).
  - World cameras are written as `E.cam3({ x, y, z, hz })`; see `S.THUMB_CAM` and sc01.

## 4 · Motion DNA
- **Camera moves:** long glides of 2–4 s through the one world (crane over the valley, the glide down to her window, along the wire pole by pole, into a window and out again).
- **Accents** land softly on the key words: a voice lifts or turns blue, a tag lights, a note glows, the phone rings, the tally rolls, the coin flips.
- **Carriers are things of the world:**
  - the voice arc carries the eye from your phone to her window;
  - the window opens into the kitchen;
  - the phone screen opens into a clip and takes it back;
  - the gold runs down the wire to the next pole;
  - the sparks from the town windows become the tally.

  Never use a fade or dissolve between worlds.
- **Nothing stands still:**
  - camera drift (`S.drift`);
  - the town windows breathing;
  - the glow pulsing slowly;
  - the moon creeping;
  - the lamp swaying on its cord;
  - her breathing and your breathing;
  - `F.paper` sliding a fine grain under everything (never over people or text).

  The kitchen itself creeps: a slow push and truck, as in sc01.
- **Figures move naturally.** She reaches for the phone with an eased IK arm and lifts it to her ear (`phone { reach, lift }` in `S.kitchen`); her head turns. You look down at your phone and up. Nothing pops and no pose snaps.
- **One wow moment per chunk**, marked ★ in shots.md. c01's is the sparks from every lit window gathering into NEARLY $8 BILLION.

## 6 · Calm rules (all chunks)
1. One motif per sentence. The picture changes with the sentence, not faster.
2. Scenes and explainers alternate. Never put two explainers of the same kind in a row.
3. At most **1–2 moving elements** at a time. The rest only breathes.
4. Each chunk is one continuous take. Inner chunk boundaries within a group are planned match frames (film.mjs only cuts; no blends).
5. Nothing appears from nowhere. Things grow out of a screen, come down the wire, rise from the windows, slide across the table, or lift off an arc.
6. Text: ≤ 6 words and at most one number per statement, readable at 390 px width, on screen ≥ 1.2 s.
7. **Subtitles are burned in at the bottom: no important text below y 880.** Source lines sit at y ≤ 880 (`F.source`).
8. **No QR overlay**, no QR window, no quiet corner for one (rule 16.11.2026).
9. The two channel moments get a **calm, readable picture** with no text but one small line:
   - M.01 in c08, 270.6–280.3 s: you on your hill, her window across the valley, the gold arc breathing;
   - A.01 in c16, 585.7–593.7 s: the glide to the end picture.
10. Numbers per chunk (half-scale review render):
    - calm.py: 0 cuts; luma 40–60; static share < 10 %; mafd median about c01's (0.55).
    - smooth.py: 0 LURCH, and a POP only at a seam frame you can name.

## 10 · Gates (motion-director-gates)
1. **Plan:** this file and shots.md exist before code.
2. **Space:** a real perspective camera through one world (`S.camPath`), with flat shapes in 3D.
3. **Carrier camera:** the camera follows the voice arc, the gold on the wire, her walk, and the clip frames growing and shrinking.
4. **Causality:** a tag lights when the gold reaches its pole; the phone rings before she reaches; the window flares when the arc arrives.
5. **5 s test:** c01 is the reference.
6. **Review frames** every 0.33–1 s plus every transition, then a contact sheet of the whole chunk.
7. **The voice drives timing:** phrase anchors only (`T.G`, `T.clip`), never seconds.
   - The ringtone follows "Her phone rings." (c01) and "her phone does ring." (c16); the phone rings visibly right after those words.
   - The hang-up follows "The line goes quiet." (c16); the blue voice dies on it.
8. **Honest self-grade** in every report.
