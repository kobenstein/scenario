## 1 · Intent
- **Audience and message:** people who work at a desk, and their parents. The machine does not take whole jobs, it takes **tasks**, one after another like dominoes; every time it takes a step, the human can take one up. The last human job is the one we won't hand over.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, ~11:17 min (VO ~11:02 + 15 s end screen), English, animated documentary, everything drawn in code, no AI images.
- **Mood:** calm, clear, hopeful under a serious night sky. One warm light that stays.
- **Explainer share 60–70 %** (weighted per second of picture: EXPL 1 · ROW .6 · STUDIO .5 · MIX .5 · SCENE 0 · CLIP 0). Current plan: **62.7 %** (see shots.md §1).

## 2 · The world
**The guiding image is thumbnail A:** a row of tall domino stones on a night plain, receding to the right; the near stone is made of warm light; a small figure stands beside it.

Three places, one night:
1. **THE ROW (anchor only):** used at the hook, the "tasks" turn (5.09), No. 1 (9.x), act 4 (10.01) and the ending (11.x).
   - Every stone is a **task**, carrying a task glyph on the upper half of its face, with a domino bar and pips below. This makes it read as a domino, not a monolith.
   - Stones fall far → near with a computed cascade (`F.cascade`): heavy, accelerating, each one leaning on the next.
   - When a stone falls, its glyph turns cool and a cool **agent bead** lifts off it: the machine has taken that task.
   - The stone of light stays.
   - Two rows exist on the plain: ROW1 (the hook, the viewer's row) and ROW2 (Jakob's steps, TOPIC of light near). Only one is drawn at a time (`S.rowWorld`, `rows: 1 | 2`).
2. **THE RANK ROW (the countdown, NOT a clock):** ten numbered stones further down the same plain, No. 10 far … No. 1 (light) near. On "Number N", stone N tips to how far that job falls (`F.RANKS[].fall`; 10–8 fall flat, 5 tips halfway, 3 trembles, 2 barely moves, 1 turns to light).
   - The **rank rail** (top left, `F.rankRail`) repeats it in miniature for the whole of acts 1–3 and shows "No. N".
3. **THE STUDIO (Jakob's thread, every [JAKOB Jn]):** Jakob (illustrated, indigo, headphones, no face, never a photo) at a standing desk under a warm lamp.
   - Behind him is the **timeline wall**: nine lanes, bottom → top MUSIC, SOUND, ANIMATION, EDIT, SHOOT, STORYBOARD, SCRIPT, IDEA, TOPIC, with track headers, a ladder and three ledges.
   - Hand-made clips are amber. When an agent bead sweeps a lane, it turns cool and keeps running it.
   - Each time a lane is taken, Jakob climbs one ledge (J3 → 1, J4 → 2, J5 → 3, conducting). TOPIC stays his (J6).
   - The year ruler 2011–2026 (15 YEARS; the last 4 YEARS stretch and turn cool) appears in the hook.

**Explainers** live in the sky over the plain or on the stones themselves, and are made of film things:
- slabs (lying stones) as bars (`F.slabBars`);
- mini stones as grids and counters (`F.stoneGrid`), including cool dashed outlines for COULD;
- light lines (`F.chartLines`: amber = people, cool = the machine, with a bead at the head);
- serif numbers, quote cards (`F.QUOTES`) and day tiles.

**Scenes** (junior desk, voice booth, radiologist with surgeon, teacher and child, doctor at the bedside, flooded basement vs clean factory, nurse at night, subscribe ridge) open as a **lit window in a stone face or a lamp**. The camera flies in, ≥ 2.5 s continuous, and back out. Each scene keeps one warm light.

**Doors between places:**
- A **light-flood door** is a push into a warm light (the stone of light, the desk lamp, a window): flood, then out of the matching light on the other side (`E.lightFlood`, both sides draw a = 1 at the peak).
- These doors are the only **hard world changes (W)**: 10 in the film, 0.89 per minute (shots.md §1).

## 3 · Nicht wiederholen (do not repeat)
No motif may be copied from an earlier film (Jakob, binding). Banned here:
- **No big clock or dial** (the ageing film), no hands, no ring that ticks, no countdown digits on a clock face. The countdown is the rank row + rank rail.
- **No hourglass**, no sand thread, no rock edge (Film 13).
- **No server rack, no Petri dish, no drawer** (Film 19).
- **No fire circle under the Milky Way.** No **world map with pins and iris medallions** (Film 24); no ear trumpet / gold listener, no stone tablet with slots, no call strip, no [translating…] bar (Film 24).
- **No kitchen table with four generations** (the ageing film).
- **No running track**, no game board, no zero line, no loop road (Film 18).
- **No monolith:** our stones always carry a domino bar and pips or a number, and stand in a row.
- **No staircase of light** and no **book of light with threads into the head** (Film 16). Jakob rises on a ladder to ledges of a timeline wall, never a glowing staircase. "Every time the machine took a step, the human took one up" is shown with lanes and ledges, not stairs.
- **No curve with a red line.** Lines are amber (people) or cool (machine) light; numbers are serif.
- **No robots as the face of A.I.** The machine is a cool bead with a ring. Exception: the factory robot of shot 7.11, small, a silhouette at work, never a face.

**Our own world (new):** the task-domino row, the stone of light, the rank row + rail, the timeline wall with ledges and agent beads, the year ruler, the light doors.

## 4 · Motion DNA
- **Energy:** calm editorial night. Long glides (2–4 s) against a few hard accents (a stone landing: clack + tiny bounce).
- **Easing:** `sineIO` for moves; `F.camPath` glides through its keys without stopping; `expoIn`/`expoOut` only for dives into a light; `rise` for text.
- **Physics:** heavy stones, with gravity and acceleration, falling far → near; light beads float. A stone starts falling when the farther one hits it (computed, not hand-timed).
- **Shape language:** flat shapes in 2.5D depth cards (`E.card`, `F0` = 1700). Every stone sits on its own depth card, so the parallax is true.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif for numbers and the rank "No. N"; Inter 600 caps for labels. The title is Inter 600 caps.
- **Camera:** follows the carrier (the bead, the cascade wave, the lit header). One main movement at a time; `camDrift` always on.
- **Finish:** `F.PRINT25 = { grain 14, floor 4, vig .5 }`. The hero light is the brightest thing.
- **Signature move:** the cascade of task stones that stops at the stone of light, while beads lift off the fallen ones.

## 5 · Palette and colour code (never break it)
| meaning | colour | where |
|---|---|---|
| human, what stays, the hero light | warm gold / amber (`P.core0`, `P.halo0`, glyph amber = mix(`P.halo0`, `P.coat`)) | the stone of light, Jakob's lamp, hand-made clips, amber glyphs, people's lines |
| the machine, a taken task | cool blue-cream (`F.COOL`) | agent beads, cool clips, cool glyphs, cool lines, dashed COULD outlines |
| night, world | indigo / blue (`P.night*`, `P.ind*`, `P.blue*`), stones `F.STONE` | sky, plain, stones, walls |
| the viewer only | yellow coat `P.coat` (`E.du`) | "you" at ROW1, the rank row, act 4, the ending |
| Jakob | indigo silhouette with headphones (`F.jakob`), never yellow | studio, ROW2 |
| text | cream; secondary `P.halo2` | labels, sources |

Deviation from the brief, on purpose: agents are **cool**, not gold. Scenario's colour code says machine = cool and human = warm, and gold is what stays. `F.COOL` is a single constant if this must change.

## 6 · Calm rules (all chunks)
- At most **~1 hard world change per minute** (only the W shots in shots.md). Every boundary is carried by an object.
- **One main movement at a time.** Camera moves are slow (≥ 2 s for big ones) and keyed on words.
- **Nothing ever stands still.** Keep `camDrift`, breathing figures, running beads and drifting stars. calm.py static share < 5 % is the target.
- **Nothing fades in half-transparent from nowhere.** Text rises from its baseline (`maskLine`/`rise`). Shapes grow from a point or come out of a thing.
- **Figures are alive:** they breathe and move their heads. Jakob types, climbs hand over hand and conducts with natural arms.
- After an accent, hold 1.5–3 s with a visible push.
- **Subtitles** own y > 910 on screen. Lower thirds and source lines sit at y ≈ 850–880.
