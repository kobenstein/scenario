## 1 · The film in one line
The people building AI are building bunkers. What are they hiding from? Four suspects on one list — the bomb (real, but old), the
machine (no door stops it: crossed off), the chaos (a bunker carries you through it, for a while) — and the fourth is written into the
floor plans: **us**. Then: a world nobody wants to storm can't be poured in concrete; you can only build a world where nobody needs one.
The motor of the picture is **THE LIST** (a suspect card, never on a door); it returns each time the question does and ends with row 4 circled.

## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise: long soft eases (sineIO for glides, quartIO for morphs, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0; ≤ 2 hard accents per 30 s (the lid falling shut on "only opens for a few" is one), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph** — an object becomes the next (slope → line; hatch lid → list card; list → board → quote card; door → dot → island outline; section → clock; plan → evidence card); (2) **cut-away** — the ground opens along the line and the next picture lives below it (bunker sections, shelters); (3) **grow-out** — a thing rises out of the line and sinks back into it (glass room, disc, houses, data centre, the two cards). A hard cut only where shots.md has **W**, and then over a rhyming shape (the gold dot; the resort window).
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects sink into the line, fold, or leave right; never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms by reading order or outward from the impulse; a group never longer than ~0.8 s.
- **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things; ambient (lantern flicker, stars, breathing light, figures breathing, the disc's rings) does not count.
- **Camera:** the view (`X.viewPath`, monotone, log scale) moves only with a reason: follow the walker / the disc / the list, push into the cut, pull back for a reveal. `S.breathe(t)` + the global float keep every frame alive.
- **Typography:** title Inter Black condensed (`F.title`, frame 0 only, sinks on "Think"); statements Instrument Serif (`X.big`); labels Inter caps 22–36 px (`X.label`); tags (`X.dateTag`); sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`); ≤ 6 words + one number; "AI" / "AGI" on screen, never "A.I.". Nothing important below y 880 (subtitles).
