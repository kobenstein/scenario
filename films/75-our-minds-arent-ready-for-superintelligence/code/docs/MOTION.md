## 3 · Motion language (locked)
- **DNA:** editorial-night, calm-precise (premium/editorial cell): long soft eases (sineIO for glides, expoOut for landings, expoIn for exits), overshoot ≈ 0 except THE GAP's spring (★ H.16), a few hard accents on the key word (≤ 2 per 30 s), then ≥ 0.8 s calm.
- **Base unit:** 0.4 s (labels rise 0.6–0.8 s, objects land 0.5–1.2 s, morphs 1.0–1.6 s, exits 0.4–0.6 s).
- **Transition family (only these):** (1) **morph**: one object becomes the next (the sphere → the gold disc; the stone head → the flint; the bar → the phone screen; a card → a clip plate; the coin → three coins); (2) **carry**: an object travels and lands in the next picture (the pulse runs left along the wall and paints the animals; the gauges fly into the HUD; the ring flies onto your chest); (3) **colour flood**: the sphere swells until gold fills the frame and shrinks into the next stage (hook only, H.02 → H.03; radius in log space, sineIO, ≥ 1 s, so it never reads as a cut); (4) **pull back / push** into a carrier (push into the phone; pull back from the sofa to the wall).
- **Camera (Gate 4):** where a carrier travels, the view follows it (look target = the carrier averaged over the last 0.2 s plus a lead along its travel, see sc01 `follow()`); otherwise the view moves only to push into or pull back from a carrier (`X.viewPath`, monotone keys, log scale). The global float (`film.mjs cameraFloat`) keeps every frame alive; holds still need ambient motion (wall light drift, breathing figures, the counter running, the ring's mark).
- **Exits:** text sinks behind its mask; objects drop, roll or fly out (the past = screen left, the future / AI = screen right); never an alpha fade of a whole picture, never a crossfade, never black.
- **Stagger:** 40–80 ms (reading order, or outward from the impulse); a group never longer than ~0.8 s. **Simultaneity:** 1–2 moving things.
- **Typography:** statements in Instrument Serif (`X.big`), labels Inter caps 24–36 px (`X.label`), sources 22 px at y 880 (`F.source`), ≤ 6 words + one number. Nothing important below y 880 (burned-in subtitles).
