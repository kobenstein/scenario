## 1 · Intent
- **Mood:** calm, curious, fair, warm in the end. The headline promise (the mammoth) is real only as *traits in an elephant*; the machine *writes a list*, people test it; the real prize is the animals still alive. Every company claim says so on screen (PER COLOSSAL, AS REPORTED, NOT PEER-REVIEWED, CANDIDATES, NOT PROOF).
- **Explainer share 60–70 %** (shots.md §1 computes it: EXPL/CARD 1 · MIX .5 · WORLD .3 · SCENE 0; currently 69.9 %). The world explains: glints, strands, the comb, the tally, the list.
- **One idea per sentence.** At most 1–2 moving elements at once (the camera counts as one).

## 2 · The world: THE FENCE (one continuous world)
- **A snowy night tundra** under stars, a low ridge on the horizon. A **wooden fence** runs across the snow (posts every 3 m, two rails, snow on every top edge; `S.POSTS`, nearer to the right). **You** (the yellow coat, hood up, `E.du`) and **a small child** (indigo, no yellow, `F.child`) stand at it hand in hand, seen from behind (`S.WORLD.you / child`). The scene is a *scenario*: SCENARIO · EARLY 2030s (`F.scenario`).
- **The field station** far left (`S.WORLD.hut`): a hut with ONE amber window (the lab, Colossal) and a mast with a small cyan light (the machine). The **lab** opens out of that window as a portal (`S.lab`: lamp, bench, the glass box with the woolly mouse, the wall screen where the comb reads two strands).
- **THE POSTER MAMMOTH** (frame 0, the hero light): a giant mammoth drawn in gold rungs of light standing on the horizon (`st.poster`, `F.beast({ light: 1 })`) — the dream, "the poster above that child's bed". In the hook its light pours down behind the fence and becomes the animal; in c15/c16 it returns as THE POSTER beside THE ARK.
- **THE ANIMAL** is ONE parametric body (`F.beast`): Asian elephant (`F.ELEPHANT`: big ears, no fur) ↔ mammoth (`F.MAMMOTH`: fur, dome, hump, small ears, long tusks). "Bringing back" = the traits moving onto the elephant: animate `fur / ears / dome / tusk / glowTrait`, never swap the drawing. **The elephant built for the cold** = `F.COLD`.
- **THE SNOW OF DIFFERENCES:** every glint in the snow is one difference in the DNA (`S.GLINTS`, 2,600 of them; `st.glint { a, lit, comb, kept, pos }`). Gold = it matters / it is lit; grey = combed out.
- **THE MACHINE** is a **cyan comb** of light: on the snow it is a line from near to the horizon with a low curtain (`st.comb { x }`), on strands and lists it is `F.scanBar` / the cyan writing of `F.list`. It reads, ranks and writes. It never touches the animal.
- **THE STRAND** is a flat ladder (`F.strand`), never a helix. Differences are gold rungs.
- **THE TALLY** (`F.tally`, top left) is the clock of the film; it only ever counts what is left: **1,400,000 → 1,600 → ~150 → 7** · the dodo **1 → 70+ → 2,300** · the biobank **~2,300 species** · the Red List **~50,000** · last **1** (a single vote).
- **THE LIST** (`F.list`): the machine's product, a short cream card written in cyan. Somebody has to test it.
- **Today's animals** (`st.wolf / ferret / bird`, `lit` = a soft gold light: an animal still here) wait in the snow behind the fence from frame 0; they light in H.15, c11, c16.
- **The ice / the freezer:** `S.iceBlock` (broken strand shards frozen in old ice; `frost 1` = the freezer door). The ice is what the mammoth left; the freezer is the ark.
- The camera is a **real pinhole camera** (`S.proj`, `S.look`, `S.camPath`, `S.drift`): sx = W/2 + (X − c.x)·s, sy = hz + (EYE − Y − c.y)·s, s = f/(Z − c.z); camera height above the snow = EYE − c.y (EYE 150). Each camera has its own horizon `hz` (a lens shift: small hz = tilted down; hz < 0 puts the horizon above the frame). Write cameras directly as `E.cam3({ x, y, z, f: F.F0, hz })` (see sc01 `C()`). Cross the fence only **above the posts** (camera height > 230 at the fence plane, z ≈ 3220 − .08·x).

## 4 · Motion DNA
- Long glides of 2–4 s through the one world (crane, push, pan, the glide back over the fence); soft accents on the key words (a glint lights, the comb passes, a trait glows, the tally rolls, an ear flicks).
- **Carriers are things of the world:** the poster's light becomes the animal, the animal becomes points, the points become strands, the gold rungs rain into the snow, the comb's picks fly into the window, the window opens into the lab. Never a fade or dissolve between worlds.
- **Nothing stands still:** camera drift (`S.drift`), snow falling (slow, sparse), glints twinkling (slow), the window light breathing, the mast blinking, the figures breathing, `F.paper` sliding a fine grain under everything (never over people or text).
- **Figures move naturally:** the hand rises with an eased IK arm (`F.reachArm` inside `S.fenceWorld`), heads turn up (`look`), the animal lifts its head, flicks an ear, breathes steam, walks with a straight-leg gait (`walk` = phase). No pops, no snapping poses.
- The wow moment of each chunk is marked ★ in shots.md. One per chunk. c01's: the whole tundra glitters (WHICH ONES?).

## 6 · Calm rules (all chunks)
1. One motif per sentence. The picture changes with the sentence, not faster.
2. Scenes and explainers alternate. No two explainers of the same kind in a row.
3. At most **1–2 moving elements** at a time. The rest only breathes.
4. One continuous take per chunk; inner chunk boundaries within a group are planned match frames (only cuts in film.mjs, no blends).
5. Nothing appears from nowhere: it pours out of the poster, breaks into points, rains from a strand, flies into the window, grows out of the snow, walks in from beyond the frame.
6. Text: ≤ 6 words and at most one number per statement, readable at 390 px width, on screen ≥ 1.2 s.
7. **Subtitles are burned in at the bottom: no important text below y 880.** Source lines sit at y ≤ 880 (`F.source`, y 870).
8. **No QR overlay**, no QR window, no quiet corner for one (rule 16.11.2026).
9. Two subscribe moments (M.01 in c08, A.01 in c16) get a **calm, readable picture**: the fence wide, little motion, no text but one small line.
10. Numbers per chunk (half-scale review render): calm.py (0 cuts; luma 40–60; static share < 10 %; mafd median as low as the picture allows — c01: see the lead's report), smooth.py 0 LURCH / 0 POP except at a planned seam frame.
