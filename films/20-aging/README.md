## Picture (lead, 2026-11-06)
- World: **THE TRACK UNDER THE CLOCK**: four generations at a kitchen table under one lamp; behind them a running track of four lanes (the
  calendar) with a movable finish line ("that day"); at its end a giant half dial rising like a sun: MONTHS OF LIFE GAINED PER YEAR
  (measured 3 → 1.7, 12 = longevity escape velocity, never reached; the hatched PUSH wedge = hope). See `docs/MOTION.md`.
- Docs: `docs/MOTION.md` (world, motion DNA, objects, palette, calm rules, carriers, kit, gates) · `docs/shots.md` (binding shot list,
  generated by `python3 tools/shots20.py --md docs/shots.md`) · `docs/BRIEF-builders.md` (B1 c02–c04, B2 c05–c07, B3 c08–c11 + end screen,
  seam plates, rules, checks).
- Timing: `film.mjs` reads `$TIMING`, else `<ROOT>audio/timing.json` (real VO), else `data/provisional_timing.json`
  (`python3 tools/provisional_timing.py`, from vo-v4.txt). `ROOT` = `$F20ROOT` or the data root above. Chunks and scenes anchor on global
  phrases: `T.G(phrase, nth, after)`, `T.GE`, `T.GW`; clips and quote cards via `T.clips`.
- Kit: `lib/f20_kit.mjs` (world constants, scene20, clock, track, table, lamp, family, eye, drop, button, proteins, wards, cards, footage frame)
  · `lib/f20_seams.mjs` (plateYou, plateEye, plateTrack; preview `node tools/seam_plates20.mjs <dir>`).
- c01 (hook + the clock) is the quality reference: `scenes/sc01.mjs`. Render: `SECS=c01 node --expose-gc render.mjs --out x.mp4 --from 0 --to 20`
  (pieces ≤ 20 s), stills `--snap a,b,c --dir d --scale .4`. Checks: `tools/calm.py`, `tools/smooth.py`, `tools/seam.mjs`.
- `audio/` belongs to the audio agent.
