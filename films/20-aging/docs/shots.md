## How to read it
- **Anchors are VO phrases**, never seconds. The scene code uses the same phrase: `T.G('phrase', 0, after)` / `T.GE(…)`. The times in the
  table are only where the phrase sits in the current timing file; when the VO changes, the code follows by itself.
- **Layer**: `[SCENE]` the world as objects (table, lamp, family, track, clock) · `[EXPL]` explainer graphics of light (on the clock face, in
  the night above the track, on the tabletop bench) · `[SCENE>EXPL]` / `[EXPL>SCENE]` the shot carries the transformation (counts half) ·
  `[CLIP]` the footage frame (counts as scene) · `[CARD]` a quote card (counts as explainer). Target infographic share 60–70 %.
- **Moves**: at most two moving things that catch the eye at once. **Transition**: "plan sequence" = the camera keeps going inside one
  world; otherwise the carrier, its screen position and its direction across the boundary. `★` = the one big moment of the chunk.
- Chunk boundaries are hard cuts on identical seam plates (`lib/f20_seams.mjs`): Lead→B1 `plateYou`, B1→B2 `plateEye`, B2→B3
  `plateTrack`. Inside a builder's range, chunk boundaries are plan sequences (the builder owns both sides).
