## 1 · Motion DNA (one cell: calm + soft, editorial)
| Dial | Film 20 |
|---|---|
| Energy | calm, 1 hard accent per ~30 s at most (needle tick, the needle's fall, a number snapping to 0) |
| Easing | sine in-out for the camera (`camAt` 's'), quart in-out ('q') only for the big moves; expo out for things that arrive; expo in for things that leave |
| Speed contrast | long glides (3–8 s camera moves) against short accents (≤ 3 frames, then ≥ 0.8 s calm) |
| Physics | heavy: the needle (falls, bounces once, settles); light: drops, sparks, cards |
| Shapes | flat 2D cards at real depth (2.5D, `stand`/`card`), grain gradients, no outlines except lines of light |
| Palette | night / indigo world, gold = human light and life, **yellow = only you**, cool blue-cream = the machine (`COOL`) |
| Transitions | camera travel inside the one world; transformation (a curve becomes the button, a frame becomes the footage); never fades, slides or wipes |
| Camera | no pitch (horizon at y 540), slow push / pull / crane, `camDrift` + the film's `cameraFloat` (off during clips) |
| Signature move | **the needle**: everything the film says about speed is read on one gauge |

## 2 · ONE world: THE TRACK UNDER THE CLOCK
A night plain. **Near**: the family's kitchen table (`TABLE`, Z 2500–2900) under one pendant lamp (`LAMP`, the hero light of the hook), four
generations seated behind it at `SEAT.Z` 3000 (X = lane centres): grandparent (gray hair) · parent · **you** (yellow raincoat) · kid; place cards
`1948` `1975` `YOU` `2019` on the tabletop. **Behind them** the start line (`START.Z` 4400) and a running track of **four lanes** (one per
generation, `laneX(i)`) running to the horizon: **the calendar** (distance = years). On the track a movable **finish line = "that day"**
(longevity escape velocity; `track({ fin })`), later smeared into a **band** (B3). At the end of the track, standing on the horizon like a rising
sun, **THE CLOCK** (`CLOCK.Z` 30000, R 9000): a half dial, the gauge `MONTHS OF LIFE GAINED PER YEAR`, 0 left · 6 top · 12 right; measured
3 (1840–2000) → 1.7 (2004–2024); 12 = the dashed threshold, never reached; a hatched **PUSH wedge** = hope, never a measurement.

Three working spaces, all inside this world (no other places):
1. **The table** (camera at sitting eye level, `camFor(0, -250, SEAT.Z, 1.0–1.1, 960, 640)`): the family, the eye, the button, cells, dishes:
   the **bench** where things are laid out (they rise out of the tabletop, `tableEye` shows how).
2. **The track** (camera behind / above the family, `cam3({ y: -20…-130, z: 900…1800 })`): who gets how far, the finish line, the band,
   the price label (B3), the levers (B3).
3. **The clock as a diagram** (camera craned up: `camFor(0, 0, CLOCK.Z, .0622, 960, 870)` → R ≈ 560 px; or flown over the family:
   `cam3({ y: -2270, z: 4500 })` → R ≈ 600 px): the sky around the dial is the explainer space (charts top left, counters top right).
The night above the track also holds the bigger explainers (the switchboard, the ward lamps on their thread, the price curve).

## 5 · Calm rules (measurable)
- **World changes:** ≤ ~1 per minute; in this film they are camera moves inside the one world (list in `docs/shots.md`).
- **Motion budget:** at most 1–2 moving main elements at once, the camera counts. Ambient life (breathing, lamp sway, stars, `camDrift`,
  `cameraFloat`) does not count.
- **Camera keys sit on words** (`camAt` keys built from `T.G`). No pitch. `camAt(t, K, CLOCK.Z)` (ref = the clock depth, so every camera z
  stays well in front of it). Hold 1.5–3 s after each accent.
- **Nothing from nowhere, nothing fading in half-transparent**: things rise out of the tabletop, lower from the dark, draw as lines of light,
  text rises from its baseline (`maskLine`, `srcLine`) and sinks again. Glows are light (allowed).
- **Never a still image > 2 s.** Text holds while read (≥ 1.5 s, ≥ characters/12 s; quote cards ≥ 4 s).
- **Bottom fifth (y > 864) stays calm**: subtitles are burned in there later (~72 px). No text, no moving accents there; static ground,
  table front or track is fine.
- **Figures are alive**: breathing (built into `gen`), heads look up on their word, natural arm poses, walking with leg phase from distance
  (`walk: (Z − Z0) · stride`), never sliding.
- **Measure:** `python3 tools/calm.py <mp4> --scale 1280:720` (cuts ≤ 0.9/min, mafd median ≤ ~0.45, luma 40–60),
  `python3 tools/smooth.py <mp4> <start>` (**0 LURCH/POP**), `SECS=cNN,cMM node tools/seam.mjs --at T0` at every chunk seam.
