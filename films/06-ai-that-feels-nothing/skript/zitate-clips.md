## Kern: Intelligenz und Bewusstsein sind zwei Dinge
**Yuval Noah Harari** · „Consciousness vs Intelligence“ (eigener Kanal, 2024) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xhy0mnD9p0U · 0:00–0:43
> "What is crucial is to understand the difference between consciousness and intelligence. Intelligence is the ability to solve problems, to win a chess, to diagnose disease, to drive a car. This is intelligence. Consciousness is the ability to feel things, like pain, pleasure, love, hate. ... we tend to confuse the two because in humans ... intelligence and consciousness, they actually go together. We solve problems through our feelings. Now, what makes AI so alien for us is exactly that. It has zero consciousness."
> 0:45–0:57: "There is a scenario that AI, as it develops its intelligence, also gains consciousness. But this is not inevitable."
Fast wörtlich Jakobs Leitgedanke. Stark für Bewegung 1.

**Anil Seth** · TED 2026 „Why AI Isn't Going to Become Conscious“ · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tJV-vdbZ388
> 2:27–2:43: "But consciousness and intelligence are different things. Intelligence is all about doing. ... Consciousness, on the other hand, it's all about feeling and being."
> 2:56–3:07: "Just because consciousness and intelligence go together in us does not mean that they go together in general."
> 3:33–3:41: "But language models are not conscious. They simulate consciousness. We project consciousness into them in the same way we might project faces into clouds."
> 9:32–9:47: "And at the heart of every experience, beneath even emotion, is this simple, shapeless and formless but fundamental feeling of being alive. ... it's life, not computation, that breathes the fire into the equations of experience."
Für Bewegung 3 (Wissenschaft). „feeling of being alive“ ist das Herz.

## Weisheitstraditionen
**Sadhguru** · „The Future of Humanity in the AI Era“ (Sadhguru-Kanal, Dez 2025) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O0OrU3IYjio · 39:11–39:40
> "Anything and everything that a human being can do from a knowledge base, a machine can do in future, including science, technology, building, teaching, book reading and ... interpretations of religion, science, everything it can do. It will not become conscious, because that is not in the mechanical sphere of life at all."
> 40:48–40:55: "The first aspect of your intelligence is called buddhi in yoga. Buddhi means intellect."
Für Bewegung 2 (Yoga: Intellekt gehört zur Mechanik, Bewusstsein nicht). Sein „will not“ ist seine Meinung, wir bleiben bei „very probably“.

**Swami Sarvapriyananda** (Vedanta Society of New York, Synapse 2024) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Co_kedLZaAo · 10:20–12:22
> "What Advaita is doing is, it's making a distinction between consciousness and mind."
> "Whatever we can label as this, that's not consciousness, that's an object to consciousness. ... This body ... thoughts, feelings, memories are also objects ... to consciousness. ... that [which] gives you the experience of all objects, that is consciousness."
Der Zeuge, für Bewegung 2 („Wer schaut da zu?“).

**Eckhart Tolle** · „Why human consciousness is nothing like artificial intelligence“ (2026) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RFZ2kdCD1jw · 5:24–6:01

## KI-Welt
**Geoffrey Hinton** · Mutterinstinkt · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmcmMsOvYeI · 2:16–2:45
> "The only method I can think of is to use the only model we have of a less intelligent thing controlling a more intelligent thing, which is a baby controlling a mother. Evolution put a lot of work into making the baby able to control the mother. And we need to put that work into making us able to control superintelligent AI by building in something like maternal instincts. If we can do that, we can survive."
(Bis „we can survive“ schneiden; der folgende Satz „if we can't do that, we won't“ ist Angst-Framing.)

**Geoffrey Hinton** · „AI Is Already Conscious“ (Alex Kantrowitz / Big Technology, Juni 2026) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV_WXmuHaiI · 0:03–0:12
