### 0 · Hook (0:00–1:01)

[calm, conversational] You can now buy a piece of A.I.
[EINBLENDUNG: Frame 0 = Thumbnail-Motiv A (riesiger goldener Lichtkristall, ein Stück bricht ab und fällt in die Hand der kleinen Figur) · groß: AI GOES PUBLIC · klein darunter: EXPECTED FIRST TRADE: THU, NOV 19, 2026 · NASDAQ: ANT]

Picture this coming Thursday. You open your banking app. And between names you've known for decades, a new name appears. Three letters. A, N, T.

Not a bank. Not a car maker. A lab that builds A.I., and nothing else.

You tap it. A price appears. And a button that says: buy.

So what exactly would you be buying?

[wry] It comes with a warning label. Not from a critic. From the seller.

Advanced A.I., the very thing it builds, could pose, quote, "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity."
[EINBLENDUNG: der ganze Prospekt-Satz mit seiner Bedingung (erst nach EDGAR-Prüfung; bis dahin nur die belegte Kernphrase "…catastrophic or existential risks to humanity"): "Sufficiently advanced AI systems could, if not developed, deployed, and operated with appropriate safeguards, oversight, and security measures, pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity …" · Anthropic, IPO prospectus (Form S-1), Risk Factors]

That is printed in its own sales document.

And the same document sets a limit. You can buy a piece of A.I. You can't buy a say in where it goes.

That stays with a group small enough to fit around a single dinner table.

Whether to buy is not this film's subject. What sits behind the button is.

So before the opening bell, it's worth knowing what the seller wrote down. The sales document, called the prospectus, is thick. More than ninety of its pages are warnings.
[EINBLENDUNG: COUNTDOWN TO THE BELL · STEP 1: THE PITCH · 90+ PAGES OF RISK FACTORS]

With most companies, you'd know the answer. The owners. Buy a share, get a vote. Buy enough, and you steer.

### 2 · Wer entscheidet danach? (ab 2:19)

[wry] Not here.

The shares on sale do carry votes. But the document caps them. All together, they stay just under half.
[EINBLENDUNG: PUBLIC SHARES (CLASS A): MAX 49.9 % OF VOTES · FOUNDERS (CLASS F): FIXED 50.1 % · Source: Anthropic prospectus]

Just over half stays with seven people: the founders. Enough, the document says, to decide shareholder votes for the foreseeable future.

And the board, which can replace the boss? Most of its seats can be filled by a trust: four people who don't own a single share. One is Ben Bernanke, who once ran America's central bank.
[EINBLENDUNG: LONG-TERM BENEFIT TRUST · 4 TRUSTEES · "AUTHORITY TO APPOINT A MAJORITY" OF THE BOARD · Source: Anthropic, Oct 21, 2026]

Seven founders. Four trustees. That is the dinner table.

So your share would be a claim on the money. It is not a hand on the wheel.

Why build a company like this? Its chief executive, Dario Amodei, told the United Nations what he believes is at stake:
[CLIP C1] "If managed poorly, I even believe that AI could be a risk to humanity as a whole."

[EINBLENDUNG (zu „best placed to guard its mission“): "…distinctly equipped to be stewards of our mission" · Anthropic prospectus, on its founders]

"…may not maximize stockholder value."

In plain words: safety may come before your return.

That is the promise. At the bell, the promise gets a share price. Can it hold?

[curious] Many doubt it. And they're saying so now, before the orders are counted.

[EINBLENDUNG: NYC Comptroller Mark Levine, letter of Oct 14, 2026]

And the four trustees? They choose directors. They don't make the daily decisions.
[EINBLENDUNG: Governance expert quoted by the Associated Press, Nov 13, 2026]

The sharpest doubt came at that same United Nations meeting, from Yoshua Bengio, who co-chairs the U.N. scientific panel on A.I. The labs, he said, admit their products could be catastrophic, yet offer no convincing fix. They say they are locked in a race. His answer:

But the company has an answer.

[EINBLENDUNG: Dario Amodei, essay "We Must Pace the Frontier", Sep 12, 2026]

That is his word, not proof. But going public did one thing no critic could: it forced the company to put it in writing, in a legal document. The risks. The losses. Who holds the votes. Anyone can read it.

So can a safety mission survive the stock market? Unproven. But from now on, it's on the record.
