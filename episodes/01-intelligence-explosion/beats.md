# 01 · Has the Intelligence Explosion Already Begun? — Fundament & Beats

## Fundament

1. **Focus sentence:** AI labs are handing the work of building AI to AI itself, because every handover makes the next one faster, **but** nobody has yet built a way to slow the process down if it goes wrong.
   *This is a story about AI starting to build AI. What's interesting about it is that the people building it are the ones asking for a brake.*
2. **Question behind the question:** Not "When does superintelligence arrive?" but "Who decides how fast the future arrives, and can we still choose?"
3. **The one insight:** An intelligence explosion isn't a bang; it's a handover you can measure. What matters isn't the speed, it's whether humans keep the power to set the pace.
   *Next headline test:* "AI wrote 80% of the code" now reads as a handover meter. The viewer asks: "And who can slow it down?"
4. **Misconception:** People picture the intelligence explosion as one dramatic moment, a switch flipping, a machine waking up. In reality it looks like a percentage creeping up in a company report, and it has already started.
5. **Guiding metaphor: the flipbook.** Each page is one generation of AI. Humans used to draw every page by hand. Now the pages help draw the next page, so the book starts flipping itself. Flip slowly and you can read each page; flip fast enough and the pages vanish, you only see motion. The solution follows from the image: a thumb on the edge of the book, the power to slow the flip. (Fits the paper-diorama look: the book is a real paper object in the 3D space.)
6. **People:** Lena, an engineer at a frontier lab (*constructed, marked*, built only from what labs have published). Real: the OpenAI security team that caught the July breach, Hugging Face engineers rebuilding the attack, I. J. Good, the TWA 514 / United crews, the Asilomar biologists, the 1,000+ lab employees behind "Pacing the Frontier". And "you" at the kitchen table.
7. **Value:** Self-determination and responsibility: the right of people to choose the pace of their own future.
