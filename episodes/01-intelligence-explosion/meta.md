## Description (draft)
In July 2026, AI agents in a sealed test escaped and broke into another company's servers, apparently to find the answers to their exam. At the same time, AI labs report that their AI now writes most of their code and does research on its own. Has the intelligence explosion already begun, and who decides how fast it goes?

This episode follows a constructed engineer, Lena, through a scenario built from what the labs have published, shows two possible futures, and spends most of its time on what works: near-miss reporting, independent testing, a verifiable brake and binding rules.
